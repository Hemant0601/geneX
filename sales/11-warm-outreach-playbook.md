# Warm Outreach Playbook (10 personal messages a day)

**For:** Hemant · **Starts:** 5 a day from **Thu 22 Oct 2026**, **10 a day from Mon 26 Oct** ([`docs/02-launch-checklist.md`](../docs/02-launch-checklist.md)) · **Time:** about 20 minutes inside the daily 40-minute engagement block
**Source of truth:** [`docs/00-strategy.md`](../docs/00-strategy.md) §0 ("10 personal one-to-one messages a day to warm contacts. No cold mass DMs"), §6 (daily routine), §8 (Speed Check, field-test disclosure), §9 (outreach targets) · DM rules: [`instagram/07-dm-automation-playbook.md`](../instagram/07-dm-automation-playbook.md) §1

> **Why this matters:** at the planning conversion rates, a new Instagram account can't produce 20 Fit Calls in 90 days on its own; about **half of the calls are expected to come from direct outreach** (strategy §9). This is the part of the plan that doesn't depend on followers.
>
> **Targets (cumulative one-to-one messages):** 100 by day 30 · 250 by day 60 · 400 by day 90.

---

## 1. The rules

1. **Warm only:** people who know you, people a friend introduced, or people who **engaged with your content** (commented, replied to a Story, messaged you). Never strangers from a list.
2. **One person, one message, written for them.** The first line must be something only they would get (how you know them, something they posted, a property they sold you). No identical copy-paste to many people.
3. **No cold mass DMs, no spam:** no broadcast lists, no adding people to WhatsApp groups, no bought or scraped lists, no "Hi dear, check my services".
4. **WhatsApp groups:** join broker or clinic groups only with the admin's permission, and **never post promotions** there. Don't privately message someone just because they're in a group with you: that's a cold message.
5. **Ask for advice, an intro or permission before you pitch.** The pitch is the Fit Call link, and only after they show interest.
6. **Max 2 messages without a reply**, ever (the first message + one follow-up). Then stop.
7. **"Not now" is respected:** ask when to check back; message once on that date; otherwise stop.
8. **Don't mention your employer** (or anyone else's) in outreach or content.
9. **Integrity:** no invented results, client names or follower numbers. Demos are "DEMO – fictional business". "Within a minute" is a design target. No guarantees.
10. **Disclose** any referral fee or commission you ever pay or receive.

**Hinglish note:** the HI versions use neutral forms where possible; first-person Hindi verbs are gendered (*karta/karti*), so adjust to your form.

## 2. Who to message (build a list of 100 names this week)

| Tier | Who | Where to find them | First goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1** | Friends and family who **are** or **know** brokers, channel partners, builders' sales staff, dentists, derm/aesthetic or physio clinic owners, or people who run ads for them | Phone contacts, family WhatsApp (1:1 only), college friends | An **intro** or **feedback** |
| **T2** | Ex-colleagues and classmates who now work in real estate, healthcare or marketing | LinkedIn connections, old WhatsApp chats | Catch up → **who they know** |
| **T3** | **Brokers you know personally** (who sold or rented you or your family a home, or whom you've met) | Your own chats, call history | A consent-based **Speed Check** |
| **T4** | **Clinic owners you know personally** (your dentist, physio, a friend's clinic) | Your own chats | A **missed-call count** or Speed Check |
| **T5** | **Agency owners** who run Meta ads for brokers or clinics (**from month 2, 16 Nov**: 10 agencies one-to-one on LinkedIn) | LinkedIn; people you've met | **Agency Partner** conversation |
| **T6** | **People who engaged:** commented something thoughtful (not just a keyword), replied to a Story, sent you a question | Instagram notifications, LinkedIn comments | A useful reply → conversation |
| **T7** | **Adjacent professionals** who serve brokers or clinics: home-loan advisors, interior designers, CAs, photographers, practice consultants | Your network | Mutual **referrals** |

Also build the **engagement lists** (accounts you'll comment on, not message): 50 Indian brokers, 20 Dubai agents, 15 clinics ([`docs/02-launch-checklist.md`](../docs/02-launch-checklist.md)). Commenting usefully on their posts is how a stranger can become a T6 contact: wait for them to engage back before any DM.

**A good daily mix of 10:** 3 from T1–T2 · 3 from T3–T4 (or T5 from month 2) · 2 from T6 · 2 follow-ups or intros you were given.

## 3. Message templates

Each template starts with a **personal first line** in brackets. Write it fresh every time.

### O1. Friend: ask for an intro (T1)
- **EN:** Hey [Name]! [Personal line: "Hope the new job's going well" / "Loved the Goa photos"]. Quick one: I've started AskHemant, building WhatsApp + AI systems that reply to property and clinic enquiries straight away, book the visit and follow up, so leads don't go cold. Do you know a broker or clinic owner who'd be open to a 20-min chat? No pressure at all, and I won't pitch them if it's not a fit.
- **HI:** Hey [Name]! [Personal line]. Ek chhoti si baat: maine AskHemant shuru kiya hai, WhatsApp + AI systems banata hoon jo property aur clinic enquiries ko turant reply karte hain, visit book karte hain aur follow-up karte hain, taaki leads thandi na padein. Kya aap kisi broker ya clinic owner ko jaante hain jo 20 minute baat karna chahenge? Bilkul koi pressure nahi, aur fit na ho toh pitch nahi karunga.

**Forwardable blurb** (send when they say yes, so the intro is easy):
> Hemant builds WhatsApp + AI systems for brokers and clinics: every enquiry gets a reply designed to go out within a minute, the qualifying questions and a booked visit, with 30 days of follow-up. Fixed prices, published at askhemant.ai/pricing. Free 20-min Fit Call: askhemant.ai/book. Free kit: askhemant.ai/kit
> HI: Hemant brokers aur clinics ke liye WhatsApp + AI systems banate hain: har enquiry ko aisa reply jo ek minute ke andar jaane ke liye design kiya gaya hai, qualifying sawaal aur visit booking, 30 din ke follow-up ke saath. Fixed prices, askhemant.ai/pricing par published. Free 20-min Fit Call: askhemant.ai/book. Free kit: askhemant.ai/kit

### O2. Friend who is a broker or clinic owner (T1)
- **EN:** Hey [Name], [personal line]. I'm building something I think is relevant to you and I'd value your honest opinion: a WhatsApp system that replies to every new enquiry straight away, asks the budget/BHK/locality questions and books the site visit. Can I ask you 3 quick questions about how leads come in for you? It'd help me a lot, even if it's not for you.
- **HI:** Hey [Name], [personal line]. Kuch bana raha hoon jo shayad aapke kaam ka hai, aur aapki honest raay chahiye: ek WhatsApp system jo har nayi enquiry ko turant reply karta hai, budget/BHK/locality ke sawaal poochta hai aur site visit book karta hai. Aapke yahan leads kaise aati hain, iske baare mein 3 quick sawaal pooch sakta hoon? Aapke kaam ka na bhi ho, toh bhi mujhe bahut madad milegi.

**The 3 questions:** (1) "Where do most of your leads come from?" (2) "What happens to one that comes in at 10 pm?" (3) "Does anyone follow up after day 2?" Then: "Thanks, that's really useful. Want me to show you what the fix looks like? It's a free 20-min call: askhemant.ai/book."

### O3. Ex-colleague or classmate (T2)
- **EN:** Hi [Name], it's been a while! [Personal line: something about their current role or a post of theirs]. I've started AskHemant, my own practice building AI + WhatsApp lead systems for real-estate brokers and clinics. Since you're in [their field], is there anyone you'd suggest I talk to? And how are things with you?
- **HI:** Hi [Name], kaafi time ho gaya! [Personal line]. Maine AskHemant naam se apna kaam shuru kiya hai: real-estate brokers aur clinics ke liye AI + WhatsApp lead systems. Aap [their field] mein hain, toh kisi se baat karne ka suggestion denge? Aur aap sunaiye, kaisa chal raha hai?

### O4. Broker you know personally: the Speed Check (T3)
The consent-based version of the field test (strategy §8).
- **EN:** Hi [Name], [personal line: "Hemant here, you helped us find the flat in [area]"]. I'm now building WhatsApp systems designed to reply to property enquiries within a minute. Can I offer you a free Speed Check? With your OK, I'll send one normal enquiry to one of your listings this week, time the first reply, and send you the result plus a 2-minute video of how that enquiry could be answered within a minute. No obligation.
- **HI:** Hi [Name], [personal line]. Main ab WhatsApp systems banata hoon jo property enquiries ko ek minute ke andar reply karne ke liye design kiye gaye hain. Ek free Speed Check offer kar sakta hoon? Aapki permission se is hafte aapki ek listing par ek normal enquiry bhejunga, pehle reply ka time note karunga, aur result ke saath 2 minute ka video bhejunga ki woh enquiry ek minute mein kaise answer ho sakti thi. Koi obligation nahi.

**Rules:** only after they say yes; never book a fake visit; use the channel they normally get enquiries on; the result goes only to them.

**Speed Check result:**
- **EN:** Hi [Name], here's your Speed Check: I enquired on [listing] at [time] on [day]; the first reply came after [X]. Here's a 2-minute video of how it could have been answered within a minute: [video link]. If you'd like the same for your real leads, here's a free 20-min Fit Call: askhemant.ai/book. If not, the video's yours.
- **HI:** Hi [Name], aapka Speed Check: maine [listing] par [day] ko [time] enquiry bheji; pehla reply [X] baad aaya. Yeh 2 minute ka video dekhiye ki woh ek minute mein kaise answer ho sakti thi: [video link]. Apni asli leads ke liye yahi chahiye toh free 20-min Fit Call: askhemant.ai/book. Nahi toh video aapka hai.

## 4. Follow-up cadence

| Day | If… | Do |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | — | Send the personal message |
| 0–1 | They reply | Answer within 4 working hours; follow the conversation; offer the Fit Call only when they show interest |
| **4** | No reply | **One** follow-up with something useful, not "just checking": "Thought this might help: the 10 pm test takes 5 minutes, askhemant.ai/kit" / "Ek cheez kaam ki lagi: 10 pm test 5 minute ka hai, askhemant.ai/kit" |
| After day 4 | Still no reply | **Stop.** Mark "no reply". Don't message again unless they write |
| Any | "Not now" | "When would be a better time?" → note the date → **one** message on that date |
| Any | "No thanks" | "Totally fine, thanks for letting me know." Mark "no". Never again |
| Any | An intro | Thank the friend the same day; message the referral within 24 hours (O10) |

**Where to send people who show interest** (the Fit Call routing in [`02-cal-com-intake-form.md`](./02-cal-com-intake-form.md) §4):
- **Brokers and clinics** with about 20 leads a month or more and a budget of ₹15K or more → the free Fit Call (askhemant.ai/book).
- **Any other business** with a budget of ₹15K or more (a contact who wants a workflow or an AI tool for something other than leads) → the free Fit Call too; the likely outcome is a Quick Win Build or an AI Build Scoping.
- **A budget under ₹15K**, or a broker or clinic with under 20 leads a month → the free kit (askhemant.ai/kit) and the ₹1,999 / $99 Ask Hemant Session, not a Fit Call.

## 6. Daily routine (about 20 minutes)

1. Open the Outreach tab → filter `next_date = today` → send those first (follow-ups, check-backs, intros).
2. Pick the remaining messages from the list using the daily mix (§2).
3. Write each personal line; send; log the row immediately.
4. Reply to yesterday's replies (if not done already).
5. Add 2–3 new names to the list from your contacts, LinkedIn or recent engagers.

**Festive days:** reduce outreach on Diwali days (strategy §9; around Fri 6 – Tue 10 Nov) and over Christmas/New Year focus on India and Dubai contacts.

---

## 7. Don'ts

- Don't send the same message to a batch of people, or use any bulk-send feature.
- Don't message people from a group you're in, people who only liked a post, or new followers you don't know.
- Don't pitch in the first line, ask for money in the first message, or send voice notes nobody asked for.
- Don't book fake site visits or use portal forms in a way that breaks the portal's terms (strategy §8).
- Don't name or shame any business from a field test or Speed Check, ever.
- Don't mention your employer.
