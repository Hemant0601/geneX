# Proposal Template (1 page) + 3-minute Loom Script

**For:** Hemant · **When:** within **24 hours** of the Fit Call (or the Audit Review) · **Valid:** **7 days** · **Tools:** Google Docs → PDF + Loom (free plan: 5-minute videos, reported) ([`docs/03-accounts-setup-guide.md`](../docs/03-accounts-setup-guide.md) §17)
**Source of truth:** [`docs/00-strategy.md`](../docs/00-strategy.md) §4 (offers, running costs) and §5 (stage 8: "the problem in their words, the outcome, scope and an out-of-scope list, milestones, price, running-cost estimate, terms"); prices and splits: [`04-pricing-sheet.md`](./04-pricing-sheet.md); legal terms: [`05-consulting-agreement-template.md`](./05-consulting-agreement-template.md)

**How to use:**
1. Copy everything between the two `✂` lines into a new Google Doc. Keep it to **one page** (A4, 10–11 pt Inter or Arial, narrow margins). Delete every `[instruction]`.
2. Pick the scope block for the offer (§2). The default proposal is the **Solo Sprint** with two options: **A = Sprint**, **B = Sprint + Lead Desk Care** (strategy §5).
3. Fill the running-cost box with **their** volumes. Never leave it blank.
4. File → Download → PDF → name it `AskHemant-Proposal-[Client]-[AH-P-2026-NNN].pdf`.
5. Record the 3-minute Loom (§4), then send both with the email in [`07-follow-up-templates.md`](./07-follow-up-templates.md) §2.
6. Create the per-client deposit link (Razorpay Payment Link, or a Skydo invoice abroad) that **expires with the proposal**.

## 4. The 3-minute Loom script

Record with the proposal PDF on screen and your camera bubble on. Speak, don't read. Free Loom is reportedly capped at 5 minutes, so 3 minutes fits.

| Time | Screen | Say (EN) |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Proposal, top | "Hi [Name], it's Hemant. As promised, here's your proposal. Three minutes, and then the decision's yours." |
| 0:15–0:45 | Box 1 | "On the call you said, quote, '[their words]'. You get about [n] leads a month from [sources], and the two leaks I'd fix first are [leak 1] and [leak 2]." |
| 0:45–1:30 | Box 2 + a simple flow sketch (a slide or a drawing) | "Here's what we'd build. A lead comes in from [source]. Within the design target of a minute, it gets a WhatsApp reply, the qualifying questions, and a tag. Hot leads ping [agent] on WhatsApp. The visit gets booked with reminders the day before and 2 hours before, and if they go quiet, the follow-up runs for 30 days. Everything lands in [Sheet/CRM], and you get a digest at 8 every morning." |
| 1:30–2:10 | Box 3 | "Two options. A is the Sprint: ₹[34,999], half to start and half at go-live. B adds Lead Desk Care after the 14-day warranty, ₹[9,999] a month, so someone's watching it and tuning it. Not included: [2 items from their out-of-scope list]." |
| 2:10–2:35 | Box 5 | "Running costs are on your accounts. For your volume I estimate about ₹[ ] a month in WhatsApp fees, plus your provider plan. That's an estimate; I'll show you the real numbers after the first month." |
| 2:35–2:50 | Box 4 | "Timeline: 10 working days from when I have access. Day 5 you see it working; day 10 it's live. If I'm late, your first month of Care is free." |
| 2:50–3:00 | Box 7 | "If it looks right, just reply A or B and I'll send the agreement. The proposal's valid until [date]. Any questions, message me. Thanks, [Name]." |

**HI version of the opening and close** (if the client speaks Hinglish; keep the screen in English):
- 0:00: "Hi [Name], Hemant here. Jaisa kaha tha, aapka proposal. Teen minute, phir decision aapka."
- 2:50: "Theek lage toh bas A ya B reply kijiye, main agreement bhej dunga. Proposal [date] tak valid hai. Koi sawaal ho toh message kijiye. Thanks, [Name]."

**Don't:** show a real client's leads or chats, show prices other than theirs, or say "this offer ends…" (only the 7-day validity and the real founding close date are deadlines).
