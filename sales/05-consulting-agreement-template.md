### 9. AI output
9.1 The system uses AI models that **can make mistakes, misunderstand questions or give incomplete answers**. Each assistant is scoped to your business and hands over to a person when it isn't confident, but no AI is error-free.

9.3 AI replies must not give medical, legal or financial advice without human review. You remain responsible for decisions made on AI output and for what is said to your customers.
