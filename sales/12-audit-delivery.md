### A2. The consented timed-test protocol

**Only with field 5 ticked, only on the ticked channels, only between the agreed dates.** Strategy §8 rules apply: never book a real visit or appointment slot, and never use a portal form in a way its terms don't allow (if in doubt, skip the portal and test the channel its alerts land in, such as the WhatsApp number on the listing).

1. **Plan:** at most **3 channels × 3 tests**. Per channel: one in business hours, one after hours (for example 22:00 IST), and one on a Sunday if the dates allow.
2. **Identity:** your own first name, and a test number and email you control. Keep the story to a normal enquiry: no invented medical conditions, loan approvals or urgent deadlines.
3. **Enquiry text:** a normal, non-committal question a real customer would ask (brokers: "Is the 2BHK in [area] still available? What's the price?"; clinics: "Do you have a slot for a cleaning next week?"). Same text on every channel, so the replies compare.
4. **Who knows:** agree with the owner in the workshop whether the team is told in advance. Default: the owner knows; the team is told after the tests close.
5. **Record each test** in the sheet below, to the minute (IST), from your phone's timestamps.
6. **Wait 48 hours** for a follow-up, then **disclose** to whoever replied (owner's choice of wording): *"Thanks for the quick reply! This was a timed test enquiry agreed with [Owner] as part of their Lead Leak Audit, so no need to follow up."* If a visit or appointment was offered, decline it the same day.
7. **Report** the results as **"timed tests (n = [x])"**, separate from any baseline. Nobody on the team is named or blamed; the result goes only to the owner.
