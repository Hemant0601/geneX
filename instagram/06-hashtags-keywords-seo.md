## 1. Keyword map

**How to read it:**
- **Primary** phrases go in a caption's first line and the on-screen hook.

- **Intent:** *Buyer* = owners who might pay. *Partner* = agencies and vendors (fine, since they're a channel). *Learner* = people who want to build it themselves (avoid as primary).
- **Portal and brand names** (99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com, Property Finder, Bayut, Meta, WhatsApp) appear only *descriptively*, as lead sources or platforms. Never use them as hashtags and never imply a partnership.

## 3. Caption SEO formula

Put the keyword in **5 places** on every post. Instagram hasn't confirmed which ones its search reads, so cover them all:

| # | Place | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Caption, line 1** (about 125 characters show before "more") | `[Primary keyword phrase]: [specific outcome or scene] ([DEMO – fictional business] if it's a demo).` Natural language, no stuffing |
| 2 | **On-screen text, 0–3 s** | The hook, in owner words. It can be the plain-language version of the keyword ("Your portal lead is in your email") |
| 3 | **Spoken, within 5 s** | Say the keyword phrase aloud once ("WhatsApp automation for real estate…") |
| 4 | **Cover title / slide 1** | 6 words or fewer, the same promise |
| 5 | **Alt text** (carousel slides) | §4 formula. Reels have no documented alt-text field, so 1–4 carry the keyword there |

**Caption template:**
```
[Line 1: primary keyword + outcome] (DEMO – fictional business)

[Line 2–4: what happens, in 2–4 short lines, one secondary keyword used naturally]
[Honesty line where relevant: "Within a minute is my design target, measured from when the lead reaches the system." / "Prices exclude GST."]
[Source line if a statistic appears: "Source: Planet DDS 2025 via DOCS Education (US data)."]

Comment LEAKS and I'll DM you the Lead Leak Kit. Check your message requests.

#tag1 #tag2 #tag3 #tag4 #tag5
```
Also add a **location tag**: {{CITY}} for India posts, Dubai for UAE posts.

## 4. Alt text: formula and examples

**Formula** (aim for about 100 characters; add detail only if the app accepts more):
```
[What it is]: [what it shows], [for whom / keyword]. [DEMO – fictional business, if a demo.]
```
- Describe what a visually impaired reader needs: the slide's message, not its colours.
- Use the keyword once, naturally.
- Don't start with "Image of".
- No hashtags, no emoji.
- Where to add it: when posting, **Advanced settings › Accessibility › Write alt text**, one entry per carousel slide (the path is unverified and may differ in your app). It can also be edited after posting.

## 5. Hashtag sets (3–5 per post; the cap is 5)

**Rules:**
- Use **one set per post, 3–5 tags**, and rotate sets. Never paste the same 5 on every post.
- **At most one city tag**: replace `{{CITY}}` with the lowercase city name and no spaces, for example `#{{CITY}}realestate` → `#punerealestate`.
- **Before a tag's first use, check it in Instagram search.** Skip it if:
  - it shows "Recent posts are hidden" (a sign of a restricted tag);
  - its top posts are spam, unrelated or mostly from other countries;
  - it's a brand name (Meta, WhatsApp, a portal or a competitor).
- Tag popularity was **not** measured. Swap any tag that fails the check for a nearby one from the keyword map.
- Hashtags go at the end of the caption, not in a comment, so they're part of the post's text.

## 6. What to avoid

| Avoid | Why |
|---|---|
| More than 5 hashtags, or 30-tag blocks | Instagram capped hashtags at 5 per post in Dec 2025 |
| Generic tags: `#ai #business #love #instagood #reels #viral #trending` | They attract everyone except buyers |
| Engagement bait: `#follow4follow #likeforlike #f4f` | Spam signals; the wrong audience |
| Learner-magnet tags: `#aiagency #makemoneyonline #sidehustle #n8ntutorial #manychat` | Attracts builders and students, not owners (strategy §10 risk) |
| Brand tags: `#whatsapp #meta #99acres #propertyfinder #bayut` | Implies affiliation; consumer-heavy |
| US-centric real-estate tags: `#realtorlife #realtor #justlisted` | The wrong market (India/UAE brokers say "broker", "agent", "channel partner") |
| Health-claim tags for clinics (`#paincure`, treatment-result tags) | No health claims; DPDP care |
| Keyword stuffing in line 1 ("WhatsApp automation real estate WhatsApp bot real estate…") | Reads as spam; hurts trust |
| Hidden keywords (white text, tiny text) or the same caption on every post | Spammy, and Instagram's originality rules penalise repeated, unoriginal content |
| Location spam (tagging cities you don't serve) | Misleading; the wrong leads |
| Hype words in captions or alt text: "guaranteed", "10x", "revolutionary", "game-changer", "never miss a deal again" | Brand voice ban |

## 7. Monthly SEO check (15 minutes, first Sunday of the month)
1. **Instagram search suggestions:** type each primary phrase from §1 (for example "whatsapp automation real", "dental clinic remind…") and note the suggestions. New buyer phrases go into the map, and dead ones come out.
2. **Google:** search `site:instagram.com askhemant` and `"askhemant" whatsapp automation`. Are posts being indexed? Which captions show up?
3. **Profile check:** the name field and bio still match `01-profile-setup.md`; the account is public; links work and carry UTM tags.
4. **Tag check:** re-run the §5 check on the 5 tags you used most. Replace any that now show hidden recent posts.
5. **Results:** in `content-tracker.csv`, compare reach and profile visits for posts whose first caption line led with a primary keyword against those that didn't. Keep what works.
