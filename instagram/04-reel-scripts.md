# Reel Script Bank: R01–R20, 40 Extra Hooks, Production Checklist

**Account:** @askhemant · **Owner:** Hemant Kumar (they/them) · **Written:** Sun 18 Oct 2026
**How to use it:**
- These 20 scripts are swap-ins and the source for weeks 5–8. The 30-day calendar ([`03-content-calendar-30-days.md`](./03-content-calendar-30-days.md)) already carries beat sheets for P11–P23.
- When a script goes into the calendar, give it the next post ID (P24, P25 …) and add a row to [`content-tracker.csv`](./content-tracker.csv).
- Pull 2–3 scripts into each Saturday batch session.

**Rules that apply to every script** (strategy §6, §8; brand voice §8):
- **One idea per Reel.** 15–45 seconds, demos 20–40. The hook lands in the first 1.5–3 seconds, with the promise burned in on screen.
- **India Reels:** Hinglish voiceover with **English** on-screen text and subtitles. Never mix languages inside one on-screen line. **Dubai and international Reels:** English throughout.
- **Every demo:** amber **DEMO – fictional business** badge top-left (inside the safe zone, §4.2), on every frame and on the cover, and "(DEMO – fictional business)" in the caption.
  - Demo businesses are **Skyline Realty – DEMO** and **SmileCare Dental – DEMO**. Every lead, patient, phone number and price inside a demo is fictional.
- **The stopwatch shows the real recorded time.** "Within a minute" is the design target, measured from when the lead reaches the system. Never speed up the timer, and never cut the wait out of a timed segment.
- `[__]` means a real, measured number. Scripts that need one carry a **Prerequisite** line. If that number doesn't exist yet, the script waits.
- **One CTA.** The default line: "Comment LEAKS and I'll DM you the Lead Leak Kit. Check your message requests."
- Hashtag set codes (RE-1, DXB-2, CL-3, GEN-4 …) are defined in [`06-hashtags-keywords-seo.md`](./06-hashtags-keywords-seo.md). Use at most 5 tags.

## 4. Reel production checklist

The Saturday batch session produces 3 Reels, 2 trial-reel hooks and 2 carousel scripts. Work through this list top to bottom for every Reel.

### 4.1 Before you record
- [ ] **One script card per Reel:** hook, beats and CTA. If the idea needs "and also", it's two Reels.
- [ ] **The hook is the first frame.** No "Hi, I'm Hemant", no logo intro.
- [ ] **Demo data is fictional and looks it:** fictional names (Rohit, Kavya, Anjali, Sara), Skyline Realty – DEMO or SmileCare Dental – DEMO, and test phone numbers you own. **Never** show a real customer chat, a real lead's number or another business's listing.
- [ ] **The phone is ready:** Do Not Disturb on, notifications from personal apps off, battery above 50%, clean home screen, demo WhatsApp account logged in.
- [ ] **The desktop is ready:** desktop clean, bookmarks bar hidden, browser zoom 125%, password manager and other tabs closed. Any API key or webhook URL that could appear on screen gets blurred in the edit.
- [ ] **OBS:** a vertical 1080×1920 canvas (or record 1920×1080 and reframe in Edits), 30 fps, highest quality. Test-record 10 seconds and play it back.
- [ ] **Talking-head shots:** phone at eye level, natural window light from the front, plain background, real desk. No stock footage or AI-generated faces (brand rule: real photos only).
- [ ] **Mic:** the clip-on mic 15–20 cm below your chin, in a soft room (curtains, bed, sofa). Record 5 seconds of silence first, for noise reduction.

### 4.2 Framing and safe zones (1080×1920)
- **Keep text, the DEMO badge and key UI inside the safe area.**
  - Stay about **270 px clear of the top, 670 px of the bottom, 120 px of the right edge and 65 px of the left**. That leaves a box of about 895×980 px starting 270 px from the top.
  - These are third-party figures. Several guides credit the 14% top / 35% bottom split to Meta, but it isn't confirmed ([Hopper HQ](https://www.hopperhq.com/blog/instagram-reel-size/), [revid.ai safe-zone checker](https://www.revid.ai/instagram-reels-safe-zone)).
  - The bottom holds the username, caption and audio; the right edge holds like, comment, share and save.
- **DEMO badge:** top-left **of the safe area** (about x 65, y 280), not the very corner, where Instagram's top bar covers it. It stays on every demo frame and is never cropped.
- **Stopwatch pill:** top-right of the safe area, in JetBrains Mono, on an amber pill with Ink text.
- **Zoom:** about 1.25× on the action (the message arriving, the timer stopping). Don't zoom past legibility.
- **Before → After:** split screens put Before on the left (Leak Red tones) and After on the right (Replied Green tones and the timer).

### 4.3 Burned-in captions
- [ ] **Always on.** The Reel has to work with the sound off.
- [ ] **Style:** white Inter SemiBold on an Ink box at 80% opacity. If Edits doesn't offer Inter, pick its plainest sans-serif and keep the white-on-dark box. Amber is never text on a light background.
- [ ] **Size:** at most 2 lines and about 5–7 words per line. Captions sit inside the safe area, above the bottom 670 px.
- [ ] **English voiceover:** use Edits' auto-captions, then **correct every word**, especially brand words: AskHemant, LEAKS, BHK, n8n, WhatsApp.
- [ ] **Hinglish voiceover:** auto-captions won't give you clean English. **Paste the English subtitle lines from the script** (the bracketed meanings). On-screen text stays in English, and a single line never mixes languages.
- [ ] **On-screen hook text** (the bold line in each script) appears by 0.5 s and stays at least 2.5 s.
- [ ] **Spoken keyword:** the main phrase ("WhatsApp automation for real estate", "dental clinic reminders") is said aloud within the first 5 seconds. Spoken words feed search (`06-hashtags-keywords-seo.md`).

### 4.4 Cover
- [ ] **1080×1920**, built from the template in `instagram/graphics/reel-covers/` (renderer in `tools/render/`).
- [ ] **Title of 6 words or fewer**, in the central area. The profile grid crops covers to roughly 3:4 (about 1080×1440 centred; the crop size is unverified), so keep the title inside the middle 1080×1080 to be safe.
- [ ] **The DEMO badge is on the cover** of every demo Reel.
- [ ] **Upload it as a custom cover** ("Edit cover" › add from camera roll). Don't use a random frame.

### 4.5 Audio
- [ ] **Voice comes first.** Peaks around -6 dBFS with no clipping; apply noise reduction lightly.
- [ ] **Demo and screen-recording Reels:** voice only, or very quiet music (about 10–15% of voice level).
- [ ] **Music goes on last, inside Instagram, from its library.** Creator accounts reportedly get the fuller music library. Keep the **master export voice-only**: Instagram-library music doesn't carry its licence to YouTube Shorts or LinkedIn, so the cross-posted version stays clean.
- [ ] Never use a song you downloaded from somewhere else.

### 4.6 The Edits app workflow
Edits is Meta's free video editor. Menu names change often, so these steps are general and weren't checked in the app; confirm them the first time.
1. **Import** the OBS recording and the phone talking-head clips into a new 9:16 project.
2. **Rough cut to the beats:** cut every pause longer than about 0.3 seconds, and every "so", "basically" or restart. **Don't cut inside a timed segment:** the wait between "lead arrives" and "reply sent" stays real.
3. **Zoom and reframe** on the action, about 1.25×.
4. **Overlays:**
   - The DEMO badge PNG and the stopwatch from `instagram/graphics/overlays/`.
   - Check the badge sits on every demo frame, including the first and last.
5. **Text:** the hook line by 0.5 s, then the on-screen lines from the script.
6. **Captions:** see §4.3.
7. **End card (last 2–3 seconds):** the CTA line ("Comment LEAKS → free Lead Leak Kit. Check message requests.") on Indigo, with the wordmark. Keep it inside the safe area.
8. **Export** at 1080×1920 in the highest quality, with no watermark. Save the master as `P24_master.mp4` (post ID first) in your Reels folder before sharing.
9. **Share:**
   - Share to Instagram from Edits, or upload the master in the Instagram app.
   - **Trial reels:** switch on the **Trial** toggle when sharing (professional accounts; eligibility varies, so check in the app). Trial reels show only to non-followers, with metrics after about 24 hours ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/12/10/instagram-rolls-out-trial-reels-that-arent-shown-to-a-creators-followers)). Mosseri reportedly said trials usually get *less* reach than normal Reels, so compare watch time and 3-second holds, not raw views.
10. **Before tapping Share:**
    - Custom cover.
    - Caption, with the keyword phrase in the first line.
    - 3–5 hashtags from the set named in the script.
    - Location tag: {{CITY}}, or Dubai for UAE posts.
    - Music from the library, if any.
    - Topics or "add tags", if the app offers them.
    - Schedule it, or post at the planned slot.

### 4.7 Pre-publish gate (no green, no post)
- [ ] Every demo frame and the cover show **DEMO – fictional business**, and the caption says it too.
- [ ] The timer shows real recorded time, and "within a minute" is described as a design target.
- [ ] No real personal data appears anywhere: chats, numbers, emails or listing photos. Blur anything doubtful.
- [ ] Every statistic carries a source on screen (Slate line) and in the caption. Vendor numbers are never presented as Hemant's results.
- [ ] Prices are exact (₹34,999 / $1,499 …) and say "excl. GST" in the caption.
- [ ] There is **exactly one CTA**.
- [ ] The keyword phrase appears in the first caption line, on screen in the first 3 seconds and spoken in the first 5 seconds.
- [ ] There are no more than 5 hashtags.
- [ ] The **LEAKS / PRICE automation covers this post.** Check whether your Comment to Message setup applies to all posts or needs choosing per post, and test it once from the second account after posting.
- [ ] The row in `content-tracker.csv` has its date, post_id, format, pillar, lane, hook and cta_keyword filled in.
- [ ] The words "guaranteed", "10x", "revolutionary" and "never miss a deal again" appear nowhere.

### 4.8 After publishing
- **First hour:**
  - Stay in the app and reply to every comment with something specific.
  - If a LEAKS comment didn't get its auto-DM, send the private reply by hand. Meta allows one private reply per comment, within 7 days ([Meta docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/private-replies/)).
- **Same day:** reply personally, inside the 24-hour window, to everyone who answered the DM question (script in strategy §5).
- **After 24 hours:** log reach, plays, average watch time, sends and saves in `content-tracker.csv`. For trial reels, compare them with the original's first 24 hours.
- **After 7 days:** update the row with final numbers, keyword comments, DMs, profile visits, link clicks (from UTM data in GA4) and follows.
- **Cross-post the next day:**
  - The voice-only master goes to **YouTube Shorts** and **LinkedIn** as native video.
  - Change the CTA, because comment keywords only work on Instagram. Use "Get the free Lead Leak Kit: askhemant.ai/kit?utm_source=youtube" (or `utm_source=linkedin`).
  - Details are in `05-stories-broadcast-collabs.md` §7.
