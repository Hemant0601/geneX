## 1. The daily Stories system (5+ days a week)

**Why it matters:** Stories warm up people who already follow you. They are where polls and question stickers turn quiet followers into conversations. Link stickers are open to all professional accounts (research §2). There's no reliable published benchmark for how many frames to post, so the numbers below are an estimate to test.

**The daily formula, 3–6 frames:**
1. **One interaction frame:** a poll, quiz or question sticker.
2. **One behind-the-scenes frame:** the real desk, a real build, a real number.
3. **One CTA frame:** a link sticker or a share of today's post. At most one link sticker a day, so Stories don't turn into ads.

**Minimum days:** Mon, Tue, Thu, Fri and Sun. On a crunch week, Wed and Sat can be a single frame. Never skip more than 2 days in a row, apart from the Diwali exceptions in the calendar.

### 1.1 Weekly Stories map

| Day | Frames | Sticker | What it's for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Share of today's Reel (post sticker) · a **qualifying poll** from §1.3 · "This week I'm building …" | Poll | Sort followers by lead source, volume or speed |
| **Tue** | Question box · share of today's carousel · answers to 1–2 questions from earlier in the week | Question | Collect real objections and topics |
| **Wed** | **Behind the scenes:** the n8n canvas, the OBS setup or the Sheet, blurred · "Build Log number of the day" · "What broke this week" (if anything did) | Slider or none | Proof of work; case study #0 |
| **Thu** | Qualifying poll (a different lane from Mon) · share of today's carousel · **kit link sticker** | Poll + link (`/kit`) | Lane data; kit sign-ups |
| **Fri** | **/book link sticker** with this week's real availability ("Fit Calls Tue–Thu; 2 evening slots for UAE/UK"), share of today's Reel, a weekend note | Link (`/book`) | Booked calls |
| **Sat** | **Weekly recap:** posts this week, [__] keyword comments, [__] calls, one lesson · a batch-session BTS ("recording 3 Reels today") | None, or a quiz | Consistency; real numbers |
| **Sun** | **Ask Hemant Sunday** (§2) · founding status frame ("[N] of 3 open · closes Mon 30 Nov") until 30 Nov | Question + countdown | Trust, FAQ content, honest scarcity |

**UTM on every Story link** (strategy §5): `?utm_source=ig&utm_medium=story&utm_campaign=<post_id or topic>`. For example: `askhemant.ai/book?utm_source=ig&utm_medium=story&utm_campaign=wk3-fri`.

### 1.2 Sticker playbook

| Sticker | Use it for | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Poll** (2 options; newer versions reportedly allow up to 4, so check) | Binary or near-binary qualifiers: "after 8 pm, who replies?" | Ask budget in public. Budget belongs on the Cal.com intake form |
| **Quiz** | "Guess the price" (₹9,999 / **₹34,999** / ₹79,999) or "guess the no-show rate" (with its source revealed in the next frame) | Quiz people on facts without citing them |
| **Question** | Ask Hemant Sunday; "what's one task you'd automate first?" | Share an answer that reveals who asked. Instagram reportedly doesn't show the asker to viewers, but check every frame before posting |
| **Link** | `/book`, `/kit`, `/founding`, `/pricing`, always with UTM tags | Post more than one link frame a day |
| **Countdown** | The founding close (Mon 30 Nov 2026, 23:59 IST); scheduled Lives | Invent a deadline or "offer ends tonight" |
| **Emoji slider** | Light mood checks ("How much do after-hours leads stress you?") | Use it as the week's only interaction; it qualifies nobody |
| **Mention / collab tag** | Collaborators, **with their permission** | Tag businesses from field tests, ever |

### 1.3 Qualifying polls and questions bank

Each poll maps to a fit signal from strategy §2: 40–500 enquiries a month, 2+ lead sources, WhatsApp answered by hand. Rotate them. Log the vote split in the Sunday scorecard notes.

**Real estate, India**
- "Where do most of your enquiries come from?" Portals / Meta ads / Instagram / Referrals
- "Enquiries a month, roughly?" Under 20 / 20–100 / 100–500 / 500+
- "Who replies to a new enquiry after 8 pm?" Me / A junior / Nobody till morning
- "Your first reply, honestly?" Under 5 min / Under 1 hour / Same day / Next day
- "Do you follow up after day 2?" Always / Sometimes / Rarely
- "Where do you track your leads?" Sheet or CRM / WhatsApp / My head
- "Site-visit reminders: do you send them?" Every time / Sometimes / Never
- Question box: "What's the most common question buyers ask you on WhatsApp?" The answers become new templates

**Dubai / UAE (English)**
- "Where do your viewings come from?" Portals / Instagram & ads / Referrals / Developer launches
- "Most of your buyers are…" In the UAE / Abroad
- "Who answers a lead at 11 pm?" Me / The team / Nobody

**Clinics**
- "Who answers the clinic phone at lunch?" Front desk / Nobody / The doctor
- "Appointment reminders?" WhatsApp / SMS / Phone call / None
- "Do you ask every patient for a Google review?" Yes / Sometimes / No
- "6-month recall reminders?" Automated / By hand / Not yet

**General**
- "What eats most of your week?" Replying to leads / Follow-ups / Invoices & admin / Reports
- "Tried Meta's free AI in WhatsApp Business?" Yes, it's enough / Yes, not enough / Not yet
- "Would you trust an AI to send your first reply?" Yes / With a human check / No (the "with a human check" voters are your people)

### 1.4 Following up on votes (manual, personal, inside the rules)
- **A vote is a light signal, not permission to pitch.** After each qualifying poll, pick **at most 3–5 voters** whose answers show a fit (for example: Portals + 100–500 a month). Message them personally. **These count towards your 10 daily messages.** Never automate this.
- **First message: one useful idea and one question. No link, no pitch.**
  > "Hi [Name], thanks for voting on my story. You picked 'Nobody till morning'. One free fix you can set up tonight: an away message that asks for budget and area, so you start the morning with context. Want me to send you the exact text?"
- **If they reply:**
  - Send the text (template 01 from the Kit) and the Kit link.
  - Ask what they sell and where most leads come from.
  - Carry on with the personal-reply script from strategy §5 and [`07-dm-automation-playbook.md`](./07-dm-automation-playbook.md), inside the 24-hour window.
- **If they don't reply:** stop. No second message.

### 1.5 What to measure (Sunday scorecard)
- **Weekly:** story replies, sticker taps, link-sticker taps (also visible as UTM `utm_medium=story` sessions in GA4), and conversations started from Stories.
- **Watch the drop-off:** if most viewers leave before frame 3, cut to 3 frames and lead with the interaction.

## 2. Ask Hemant Sunday

The name is the CTA ("Ask Hemant"), and Sunday has no feed post (strategy §6). Each week it produces FAQ material, Reel ideas and warm conversations.

### 2.1 Mechanics

| Time (IST) | Step |
|---|---|
| **10:00** | Post the question box on Indigo, with the wordmark. Rotate the prompts: "Ask Hemant: what should you automate first?" · "Ask me anything about WhatsApp for your business" · "What's one task you'd never want to do again?" · "Is your idea automatable? Ask." |
| 10:00–17:00 | Questions come in. Copy each one, **without the name**, into the "AHS questions" tab of the pipeline Sheet: date · question · lane (RE/CL/GEN) · answered (y/n) · became content (post ID) |
| **17:00–19:00** | Answer **5–10** questions as Stories, one per frame. Use text on Ink, a 15-second talking head, or a 20-second screen recording |
| 19:00–19:30 | **Scorecard** (calendar). The best question of the week goes into the Reel bank. Write "AHS" in the tracker notes when it ships |
| Next day | DM anyone whose question needs a private answer. Their question opens the 24-hour window |

### 2.2 Answer rules
- **Answer honestly, including "you don't need me for this".** For example: "If 90% of your chats are FAQs, try Meta's free AI first."
- **Keep identities out.** Never show a name or profile photo. If a question includes business details, generalise them ("a clinic owner asked…").
- **No medical, legal, tax or financial advice.** Point people to a doctor, a lawyer or a CA. The same goes for GST questions, beyond "talk to a CA".
- **No result promises.** "Within a minute" is a design target. Never say "you'll get more sales".
- **Every price exact and excl. GST.** If a price comes up, link `/pricing` instead of rounding.
- **One or two answers a week may end** with "Comment LEAKS on any post for the kit" or the `/book` sticker. The rest just answer.

### 2.3 From question to content
- **Every week:** at least 1 question becomes a Reel (P5 Straight Talk, Friday slot), a carousel slide or an FAQ highlight frame.
- **When 3+ people ask the same thing:** that's a pinned-post or website-FAQ candidate. Tell the website owner (FAQ on `/` and `/pricing`).

## 4. Broadcast channel (from about 300 followers)

**When to start:** at about **300 followers** (strategy §6, month 2). Research suggests 300–500+ *(estimate)*.
- Broadcast channels are reportedly open to Creator and Business professional accounts in most regions, but check the account's eligibility in the app.
- Members can read, react and vote in polls. Reportedly they can't reply in the channel, so conversations still happen in DMs.

**Name:** *AskHemant: No lead left on read*
**Description:** "One useful automation note a week for brokers and clinic owners: templates, real numbers from my own funnel, and honest takes on WhatsApp and AI. No spam, no hype."

**Launch sequence:**
1. Create the channel (Instagram inbox › new message › create broadcast channel; the path is unverified, so find it in the app).
2. Post the welcome message and the first template (below) before inviting anyone.
3. Invite people with a Stories frame on Mon and Thu of launch week, plus one caption line on that week's carousel: "Weekly notes in my broadcast channel: link in Stories."
4. Add it to the Start Here highlight.

**Cadence:** **one post a week, Thursday about 13:00 IST**, plus at most one extra in a week when something real happens: a founding slot fills, a Live is scheduled, prices change. **Never more than 2 posts a week.**

**4-week rotation:**

| Week | Post type | Example |
|---|---|---|
| A | **Template of the week** | A copy-paste WhatsApp reply from the Kit, in English and Hinglish, plus a one-line "when to send it". A poll: "Will you use it?" |
| B | **Build Log numbers** | The week's real numbers from your own funnel, 5 lines, with "what I changed" |
| C | **Field note / Straight Talk** | One rule change (for example Meta's per-message pricing, or the general-purpose bot ban), with its source link, or one lesson from a Fit Call, anonymised |
| D | **Availability + what's next** | "Fit Call slots next week: Tue 11:30, Wed 16:30, Thu 19:30 (UAE/UK)": only real, open slots. Plus the next Live date, if one is scheduled |

**Formats:** text first (5–8 lines); one image (a template card or numbers card); a poll at most every other week; an occasional 30-second Hinglish voice note for India.

**Rules:**
- No "DM for price".
- Nothing the website and feed don't say just as openly.
- No forwarded AI news.
- DEMO labels and sources apply here too.
- Measure members, views per post, poll votes and link taps (UTM `utm_medium=broadcast`).

**First three posts, ready to go:**
1. **Welcome:** "Hi, I'm Hemant. I build AI + WhatsApp systems for brokers and clinics so no lead waits. Once a week I'll share one thing you can use straight away. First up: the reply to send instead of 'Yes' when someone asks 'Is this available?' ↓"

### 5.1 Who to collab with

| Partner type | Why they fit | The angle | Example collab |
|---|---|---|---|

My system makes sure the call happens." | A joint Reel: "The 3 replies your agents should send in the first 10 minutes". They bring the script; you show it running (DEMO) |

(strategy §9) | Teardown Reel: "Your cost per lead is fine. Your reply time isn't." A DEMO before/after. Also the doorway to the **Agency Partner** offer (list price −25%) |
| **Proptech / CRM vendors and WhatsApp providers** (Indian real-estate CRMs, BSPs) | Clients ask "which tool?"; vendors want implementation stories | "What a working setup on [tool] looks like" | A carousel or Live: "How a broker's leads flow into [CRM] on WhatsApp" (DEMO). **Disclose any commission** (for example AiSensy's reported 20% recurring partner commission, `research/market-competitors.md`). Never imply an official partnership without one in writing; use their logo only with permission |
| **Dental / clinic marketing agencies and clinic consultants** | They fill the calendar; missed calls and no-shows waste it | "You fill the chairs. I stop them emptying at lunch and through no-shows." | A joint Live: "The missed-call math for clinics" (the lunch-hour test, P19) |
| **Dubai real-estate creators who teach agents** (English) | The month-2 Dubai push | "Leads at 11 pm, viewing booked by 8 am" | A collab Reel built on R06 or P20 |

**Fit criteria:**
- Their audience is **owners and agents, not would-be agency builders**.
- Real comments on recent posts.
- Roughly 2K–50K followers *(estimate; small, engaged accounts say yes more often)*.
- No income claims or "guaranteed" language in their own content.
- You'd be happy to have their name next to yours.

**What both sides agree before recording:**
- **Topic and format:** a collab Reel or carousel with one invite. Up to 5 co-authors are reportedly allowed, and every invitee must accept.
- **Claims check:** no invented numbers, DEMO labels on demos, sources on statistics.
- **One CTA:** decide whose. The default is the partner's audience gets the free Lead Leak Kit via LEAKS. **Test whether your comment automation fires on a collab post you co-own** (unverified).
- **Money:** none by default. If money or a commission changes hands, use Instagram's paid-partnership label and say so in the caption.
- **Approval:** both see the final cut before it goes live.

### 5.2 Outreach templates (Instagram DM, LinkedIn or email)

**Warm first:** for 2 weeks before messaging, leave 3–5 genuinely useful comments on the person's posts. Then send **one** message, plus **one** follow-up after 5–7 days if there's no reply. Then stop. Each one counts in your daily 10.

**T1: Real-estate trainer / brokers' coach**
> Hi [Name], I've been following your posts on [specific topic, e.g. handling "just send details" buyers]. The [specific post] one was spot on.
> I build WhatsApp systems for brokers that reply to new enquiries within a minute and book the site visit. Your training is about what to say; my work makes sure someone says it before the lead goes cold.
> Idea: one collab Reel, "the 3 replies to send in the first 10 minutes". You give the script, I show it running live (clearly labelled as a demo). Your audience gets a free template kit. No cost, no catch. Open to it?

**T2: Real-estate performance marketer / Meta-ads agency**
> Hi [Name], I like how you break down [specific campaign or teardown]. Honest question: how often do your clients' leads sit unanswered for hours after your ads do their job?
> I build the bit after the ad: instant WhatsApp reply, qualifying questions, site-visit booking, 30-day follow-up. The angle for a joint post: "you bring the leads, I make sure none leak."
> One collab Reel, a before/after demo (clearly labelled), and a free kit for your audience. If it goes well, I also have a white-label option for agencies. Worth a 15-minute chat?

**T3: Proptech / CRM vendor or WhatsApp provider**
> Hi [Name], I'm an independent automation consultant building WhatsApp lead systems for brokers and clinics on [their tool]. I publish prices and build everything on the client's own accounts.
> Would your team be open to a co-created post: "what a working broker setup on [tool] looks like", with a clearly labelled demo? I'd send you the draft first and wouldn't claim any official partnership. If you have a partner programme, happy to hear about it, and I'd disclose it publicly.

**T4: Dental / clinic marketing agency**
> Hi [Name], your [specific post about patient acquisition] got me thinking. You fill the calendar; I work on the leaks after that: missed calls at lunch, no-shows, no review requests.
> Idea: a joint Live or carousel on "the missed-call math for clinics". A 5-day test clinics can run themselves (no made-up numbers), plus the free WhatsApp fix. Your audience gets a free template kit. Interested?

**T5: Dubai creator / brokerage trainer (English)**
> Hi [Name], your content for Dubai agents is some of the most practical I've seen, especially [specific post].
> I build WhatsApp systems that answer a listing enquiry at 11 pm, qualify it and book the viewing before the agent wakes up. I'm India-based, which means I'm only 1.5 hours ahead of Dubai.
> Would a collab Reel work? "Lead at 11:40 pm, viewing booked by 8 am", a labelled demo, and a free lead-leak kit for your followers.

**Follow-up (once, 5–7 days later)**
> Hi [Name], just floating this back up in case it got buried. No worries at all if it's not a fit right now.

**After a yes: the one-page collab brief**
- **Working title:** [__]
