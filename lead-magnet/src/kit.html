The 7 leaks 01–02 / 07
01

After-hours enquiries

enquiry 10:42 pm · first reply 9:15 am

Looks likeA lead from your Meta ad lands at 10:42 pm. You see it after breakfast, once they’ve called other brokers.

Costs youSpeed is rare. In a US audit of 2,241 firms, only about 37% answered web leads within an hour; 23% never did.1

Fix this week · freeSet a WhatsApp Business away message for after hours: thank them, ask one question, say when you’ll call (01).
02

Portal leads that never reach WhatsApp

portal alert 11:05 am · first call next day

Looks likeAlerts from 99acres, MagicBricks or Housing.com (Bayut in Dubai) sit unread under OTP emails.

Costs youYou paid for that lead. While it waits, the buyer talks to whoever called first.

Fix this week · freeGmail filter: star and label every portal alert. Within the hour, open wa.me/[country code + number] and send 01.

1 BYU ScholarsArchive / HBR, “The Short Life of Online Sales Leads” (2011), audit of 2,241 US firms. scholarsarchive.byu.edu/facpub/9711 · US data; no comparable India study found.

03

Unanswered Instagram DMs

“price?” DM · opened 3 days later

Looks likeSomeone comments “price?” or DMs “details pls”. It sits in Message requests for days.

Costs youA “price?” is a raised hand. Three days later, they’ve forgotten your listing.

Fix this week · freeCheck Message requests every evening. In Meta Business Suite (Inbox › Automations), set an instant reply with your WhatsApp link.
04

No qualifying questions

15-min call · “actually, I want to rent”

Looks likeEvery enquiry gets the same long call. At minute 15 you learn they wanted a rental.

Costs youYour best hours go to the least serious leads, while ready buyers wait.

Fix this week · freeAsk the 6 questions on WhatsApp first, two at a time (templates 02–04, page 8). Label chats Hot, Warm or Cold. Call Hot first.

ClinicAsk the treatment, new or returning, and preferred time.

The 7 leaks 05–06 / 07
05

No visit reminders, so people don’t show up

visit fixed Tue · no-show Sat

Looks likeA Saturday 11 am visit, fixed on Tuesday. You drive out, wait 30 minutes: “sorry, something came up”.

Costs youA wasted trip and a blocked slot. Clinics too: Planet DDS reports a 6.9% average dental no-show rate.2

Fix this week · freeTwo reminders: 24 hours before (“reply 1 to confirm, 2 to reschedule”) and 2 hours before, with the Maps pin (templates 06–07).
06

No follow-up after day 2

2 calls, no answer · chat forgotten

Looks likeYou reply once and call twice. No answer, so the chat sinks and nobody touches it again.

Costs youProperty decisions take weeks. Quiet on day 2 can mean ready in week 3, with whoever kept in touch.

Fix this week · freeDay 2: resend template 05. Day 7 and day 30: templates 09–10, with an opt-out. Label the chat Follow-up; check that label daily.

2 Planet DDS 2025 data, reported by DOCS Education: average dental no-show rate 6.9%, cancellations 12.9% (US practices). docseducation.com/node/53377

07

Leads that aren’t logged anywhere

4 inboxes · 0 lists

Looks likeLeads sit in portal emails, Meta forms, Instagram DMs and WhatsApp. Nobody knows last month’s count.

Costs youYou can’t see which source is worth paying for, or which lead a junior dropped.

Fix this week · freeOne Google Sheet: date, name, phone, source, need, status, next step, next date. Status: New › Replied › Visit booked › Won or Lost. Two minutes every evening.

Clinic“Need” becomes “treatment”. Keep health details out of it.

Clinic: after a missed call

/call

Marketing/d30

09 and 10 are marketing messages: always keep the STOP line. Send 09 only if the options are real. After a STOP, label the chat “Opted out” and stop. Message only people who enquired with you. On the WhatsApp API, submit these for Meta approval with the [brackets] as variables.

The 10 pm self-test

22:00

Be your own buyer for one night, then score the reply.

1

Pick one live ad or listing: portal, Meta ad, Instagram post or website form.

2

At 10 pm, enquire like a buyer from a phone your team won’t know (a friend’s, with their OK): “Is the 3BHK still available?”

3

Note the exact time. Don’t warn anyone.

4

Next morning, note the first reply: time, channel, words.

5

Then go quiet for 2 days. Does anyone follow up?

What free tools won’t do
