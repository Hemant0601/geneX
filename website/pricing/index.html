How payment and delivery work.

The short version. Each build also gets a written agreement; see the terms page.

50/50 on builds

Sprints and the Team system: 50% to start, 50% at go-live. The Audit and the Session are paid 100% upfront.

Fees are credited

The Session fee is 100% credited to an Audit or build booked within 14 days. The Audit fee is 100% credited to a Sprint booked within 30 days.

Retainers

Paid monthly in advance. Care: 30 days’ notice to cancel. Growth: 3-month minimum.

The clock starts with access

Timelines count from the day the access checklist is complete. Meta verification and template approval start on day 1.

On-time promise

If a Sprint isn’t live within 10 working days of full access, your first month of Lead Desk Care is free.

Fixed scope

Every proposal has a written out-of-scope list. Changes outside it are ₹2,000/hour / $60/hour. Proposals are valid for 7 days.

Limited capacity

I run at most 2 Sprints at once, so the timeline holds, and take at most 6 Fit Calls a week.

Your accounts, your IP
