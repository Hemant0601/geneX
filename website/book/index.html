What happens on the call

  1. 0–2 min

    How the call works. By the end, you get an honest answer on fit and a clear next step.

  2. 2–10 min

    Diagnosis: your lead sources and monthly volume, who replies and how fast, what happens after the first reply, what a closed deal is worth, and what you’ve already tried.

  3. 10–15 min

    I name the 1–2 biggest leaks and recommend a next step: the Audit, straight to a Sprint if your setup is simple, or the free Kit if it isn’t a fit.

  4. 15–20 min

    Price and timeline. If the Audit makes sense, I can send the payment link on WhatsApp during the call. Otherwise you get a 1-page proposal within 24 hours.

The Fit Call is a diagnosis, not a free design session. Designing your system is what the Audit is for.

After you book: Cal.com sends a confirmation and email reminders, and I send a WhatsApp message 2 hours before. Reply 1 to confirm.
