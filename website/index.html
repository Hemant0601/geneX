FAQ

Straight answers.

Why not just use Meta’s free WhatsApp AI?

If you only need answers to common questions, use it. It’s free, and it’s good at FAQs. Meta rolled out Business AI and Meta Business Agent on WhatsApp for Indian businesses in 2026 (Deccan Herald).

What it doesn’t do on its own: pull in your portal and Meta-ad leads, send hot leads to the right agent, book the site visit on your calendar, follow up on day 7 and day 30, and log everything in your Sheet or CRM. That connected system is the part I build, and I’ll use Meta’s free tools inside it wherever they fit.

How fast will my leads get a reply?

The design target is a first reply within a minute, timed from the moment a lead reaches the system. Some sources add their own delay before that point, portal alert emails for example. I confirm every source in the Audit before I quote, and I measure your response time before and after go-live.

I won’t promise you a number I haven’t measured for your business.

How long does it take?
  • Lead Leak Audit: 5 working days.
  • Speed-to-Lead Sprint (Solo) and the clinic Sprint: 10 working days.
  • Speed-to-Lead System (Team): 15 working days.
  • Quick Win Build: 7–10 working days. Custom AI Agent / AI Product Build: 4–8 weeks.

The clock starts when the access checklist is complete. Meta business verification and template approval usually set the pace, so we start both on day 1. If a Sprint isn’t live within 10 working days of full access, your first month of Lead Desk Care is free.

Is my data, and my customers’ data, safe?

Everything is built on your own accounts: your Meta Business Manager, a WhatsApp provider account in your business’s name, and your own Sheet or CRM. I work as a data processor under a written clause, collect only what the system needs, and remove my access and copies when the contract ends.

India’s DPDP Act 2023 applies, and I design for it: clear opt-in, opt-out on every follow-up sequence, and minimal health data for clinics. For this website, see the privacy notice.

What if it breaks?

Every build comes with a bug-fix warranty: 14 days for the Solo and clinic Sprints, 30 days for the Team system. After that, Lead Desk Care (₹9,999/mo / $299/mo) covers monitoring, error alerts and fixes within 1 working day.

You also get a Loom walkthrough and a 1-page SOP, so you’re never left with a black box.

What does it cost to run each month?

Running costs are separate from my fee and are paid by you, on your own accounts: WhatsApp per-message fees, your WhatsApp provider’s plan, AI usage, and any CRM or hosting. As a worked example (an estimate), 300 new leads a month with 3 follow-ups each comes to about ₹775 a month in Meta fees, before GST and provider fees. See the breakdown.

Do you guarantee results?

No. I commit to the scope, the timeline promise and the warranty. I don’t promise deals or revenue, because nobody honest can. What I do is measure first-reply time, qualification and bookings before and after, so you can see exactly what changed.

Does it work in Hinglish?

Yes. The AI qualifier replies in English or Hinglish and is scoped to your business only. When it isn’t confident, it hands the conversation to a human instead of guessing.

Do you work with brokers in Dubai, or clients abroad?

Yes. Dubai is 1.5 hours behind IST, so our working hours overlap almost completely. International clients pay in US dollars, by invoice through Skydo or Wise Business.

I’m not in real estate or running a clinic. Can you still help?

Often, yes. The Quick Win Build (₹24,999 / $999) is one workflow for any business, such as form → CRM → WhatsApp follow-up, or invoice PDF → AI extraction → Google Sheets or Zoho Books. For bigger ideas, the Custom AI Agent / AI Product Build starts at ₹1,50,000 / $6,000 after an Audit. For a quick second opinion on anything AI or automation, book an Ask Hemant Session.

How do payments work?

Sprints and the Team system: 50% to start, 50% at go-live. The Audit and the Ask Hemant Session are paid upfront, and retainers monthly in advance. In India I use Razorpay payment links; abroad, a Skydo or Wise Business invoice (PayPal only for the $99 Session). All prices exclude GST. Full terms are on the pricing page.
