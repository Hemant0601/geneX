1. Who is responsible

AskHemant is the trading name of Hemant Kumar, an independent AI automation consultant based in India. For personal data collected through askhemant.ai and my enquiry process, I am the data fiduciary under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). You can reach me at hello@askhemant.ai.

2. The short version

  • I collect only what I need to reply to you, book a call, send what you asked for, and get paid.
  • Marketing messages need a separate, unticked box. You never have to tick it to get a reply or the kit.
  • I don’t sell or rent your data.
  • You can ask me to show, correct or delete your data at any time by emailing hello@askhemant.ai.

4. Consent and lawful use

I process your data either because you have given it to me voluntarily for a specific purpose (for example, sending a message so I can reply, or booking a call), or because you have given consent (for example, ticking the box for tips and follow-ups), as the DPDP Act allows. You can withdraw consent at any time, and it is as easy as giving it: reply STOP to any WhatsApp message, reply “unsubscribe” to any email, or email hello@askhemant.ai. Withdrawing consent doesn’t affect anything done before you withdrew it.

6. Cookies and analytics

  • Analytics: Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity load only when I have switched them on, and never if your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal. They set their own cookies (for example _ga, _clck, _clsk). Clarity records clicks, scrolls and mouse movement to show how pages are used, and masks text you type into forms.
  • Embedded tools: the Cal.com calendar and the Tally form may set cookies needed to work.
  • Your preferences: this site stores your currency choice and analytics choice in your browser’s local storage, and campaign tags (like utm_source) for the current visit only, so a booking can be credited to the post that brought you here.

Analytics on this device: On, if analytics are configured.

7. Marketing messages

I only send tips or follow-ups if you tick a separate, unticked consent box. WhatsApp follow-ups use Meta-approved templates and go only to people who opted in. Reply STOP on WhatsApp or “unsubscribe” by email at any time. I don’t add anyone to bulk or cold broadcast lists.

8. How long I keep it

  • Enquiries, bookings and calculator requests where we don’t go on to work together: up to 12 months after our last contact, then deleted.
  • Client, invoice and payment records: for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires.
  • Analytics: under each provider’s retention settings (Google Analytics is set to its 14-month maximum).
  • Marketing consent records: while you’re subscribed, and for a reasonable period afterwards to prove consent was given.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data I hold about you and how I use it, and who I have shared it with;
  • ask me to correct, complete, update or erase it;
  • withdraw consent at any time;
  • have your grievances addressed (see section 13);
  • nominate someone to exercise these rights for you in case of death or incapacity.

Email hello@askhemant.ai with the subject “Privacy request”. I may ask you to confirm your identity. If you’re not satisfied with my response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India. If you are in the UK or EU, you have similar rights under the GDPR, and I’ll honour them in the same way.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. My accounts use two-factor authentication, and access to your data is limited to me. No method is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, I will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

11. Children

This site and my services are for businesses and adults. I don’t knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent me their data, email me and I’ll delete it.

12. Client projects

When I build a system for a client, the client’s customer data stays in the client’s own accounts. The client is the data fiduciary and I act as their data processor under a written data-processing clause: minimal data, access limited to the project, and my access and copies removed when the contract ends.

14. Changes to this notice

If I change how I handle data, I’ll update this page and the date at the top. For significant changes affecting people who gave consent, I’ll tell them directly.
