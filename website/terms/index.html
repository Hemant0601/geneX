Website and engagement terms

How this website works and how I work with clients, in plain English. For any paid project, the signed proposal and agreement take priority over this page.

1. Who these terms are with

“I”, “me” and “AskHemant” mean Hemant Kumar, trading as AskHemant, based in India. “You” means the person or business using this site or hiring me. Contact: hello@askhemant.ai.

2. Using this website

  • Content on askhemant.ai is general information, not legal, tax or financial advice.
  • Demos are fictional. Businesses such as Skyline Realty and SmileCare Dental are labelled “DEMO – fictional business”. Demo timings show design targets, not client results.
  • Statistics from other organisations are cited with their source. I don’t present them as my results.
  • Links to other sites are for convenience; I’m not responsible for their content.
  • You may share the free Lead Leak Kit as it is, with credit. Please don’t resell it.

3. Prices

  • Prices are in Indian rupees for clients in India and US dollars for clients elsewhere, as listed on the pricing page.
  • All prices exclude GST. GST is added where it applies.
  • These are launch prices and may change. A change never affects a proposal you have already accepted. Proposals are valid for 7 days.
  • Running costs (WhatsApp per-message fees, WhatsApp provider plans, AI usage, CRM and hosting) are separate and paid by you, on your own accounts.

4. Payment terms

  • Sprints and the Team system: 50% to start, 50% at go-live.
  • Lead Leak Audit and Ask Hemant Session: 100% upfront.
  • Retainers: monthly in advance. Lead Desk Care needs 30 days’ notice to cancel; Lead Desk Growth has a 3-month minimum.
  • Credits: the Session fee is 100% credited to an Audit or build booked within 14 days; the Audit fee is 100% credited to a Sprint booked within 30 days.
  • Founding Client Program: ₹14,999 / $599 for an Audit plus a Solo or Clinic Sprint, 50% to start and 50% at go-live, for up to 3 clients until Mon 30 Nov 2026. It includes the written measurement and publication terms on the founding page.
  • India: Razorpay payment links. Outside India: Skydo or Wise Business invoices; PayPal only for the $99 Session.
  • If a payment is overdue, I may pause work until it’s paid. Timelines move by the length of the pause.

5. Scope, timelines and delivery

  • Every build has a written scope and an out-of-scope list. Changes outside the scope are charged at ₹2,000 / $60 an hour, agreed in writing first.
  • Timelines count in working days from the day your access checklist is complete (for example admin access to your Meta Business Manager and a WhatsApp provider account in your business’s name).
  • On-time promise: if a Sprint isn’t live within 10 working days of full access, your first month of Lead Desk Care is free. Delays caused by third-party approvals you control, or by missing access or feedback, pause the clock.
  • All services are delivered online, through video calls, shared documents and your own software accounts. Nothing is shipped.

6. Your accounts and third-party platforms

  • Systems are built on your accounts, and you remain the owner of those accounts and the data in them.
  • WhatsApp, Instagram, portals and other platforms have their own rules and can change or reject things (for example, a message template). I design for their policies, but I don’t control their decisions.
  • You are responsible for having your customers’ consent to message them, and for the content you approve.

7. No guaranteed results

I don’t guarantee leads, sales, revenue, return on investment or any specific response time. “Within a minute” is a design target, measured from when a lead reaches the system; some lead sources add delay before that. What I commit to is the agreed scope, the timeline promise and the warranty.

8. AI-generated output

  • AI assistants can make mistakes or give incomplete answers. Each assistant is scoped to your business and hands over to a person when it isn’t confident, but you should review conversations, especially early on.
  • AI replies must not be used for medical, legal or financial advice without human review.
  • You are responsible for decisions you make based on AI output.

9. Cancellations and refunds

  • Ask Hemant Session: you can reschedule free of charge with at least 24 hours’ notice. If I cancel and we can’t find a new time that suits you, I refund the fee in full.
  • Lead Leak Audit: if you cancel before the workshop takes place, I refund the fee in full. After the workshop, the fee isn’t refundable, but it stays credited to a Sprint booked within 30 days.
  • Sprints and builds: if you cancel after paying the deposit, I refund any part of the deposit not covered by work already done.
  • Retainers: cancel with the notice above. The current paid month isn’t refunded.
  • Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, normally within 7 working days.

10. Warranty and support

Builds include a bug-fix warranty: 14 days for the Solo Sprint, the Clinic Sprint and the Quick Win Build, and 30 days for the Team system, starting at go-live. It covers defects in what I built. It doesn’t cover changes or outages at third-party platforms, or changes made by others. After the warranty, support is available through Lead Desk Care or at the hourly rate.

When you have paid in full, the deliverables built for you are yours. I keep ownership of my pre-existing templates, code and know-how, and you get a licence to use any of them included in your build.

For client projects, you are the data fiduciary and I act as your data processor under a written data-processing clause: I use your data only for the project, keep it to a minimum, and remove my access and copies when the contract ends. See the privacy notice for this website.

13. Case studies

I only publish a client’s name, screens or numbers with written permission. Anonymised is the default. Testimonials are quoted word for word.

14. Liability

15. Law and disputes

16. Changes
