## 1. Hostinger pricing and rules (2026)

Hostinger sells **both .ai and .si**.

| TLD | First year at Hostinger | Renewal | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **.ai** | $89.99 (Hostinger's .ai page); $99.99 (DomainOffer tracker, Jul 2026) | **$109.99/yr** | **2-year minimum registration**, a registry rule. Expect roughly **$190–$210 up front** *(estimate)*. Not covered by Hostinger's free-domain offer. Registrants anywhere in the world can register. |
| **.si** | **$11.99** (US) · **₹1,169** (India storefront) · £9.99 (UK) | $16.99 · **₹1,649** · £12.99 | 1–3 year terms; WHOIS privacy included. |
| **.com** | $9.99 (DomainOffer, May 2026); ₹1 promo on the India storefront (Zoutons, Aug 2026) | $19.99 · ₹1,499 | Often free for year 1 with a hosting plan. |
| **.in** | $2.99–$6.99 / ₹1–₹549 depending on promo | ~$9.99 / ₹899 (one source shows ₹1,499) | Reads as India-only to foreign buyers. |
| **.co** | $27.99 (Jul 2026); $23.79 promo seen Oct 2026 | $34.99 | Often mistyped as .com. Not recommended. |

Prices come from Hostinger's pages and third-party trackers, and promos change often, so **confirm the price in the cart**. Hostinger also says that after you register, you **must verify the registrant email**.

### Can someone outside the EU register a .si domain?

- Hostinger's India .si page says **anyone can register: no residency requirement and no Slovenian company needed**.
- The registry's own General Terms (register.si, valid from 15 Nov 2024) define a holder simply as "a natural or legal person". Residence or a registered office is *collected as data* but is not listed as an eligibility condition. World Trademark Review covered the earlier relaxation that opened .si to foreign persons.
- Some third-party pages (domaindetails.com, 365i) still say you need a connection to Slovenia or the EU. **These look out of date**, but the sources do conflict.
- Under **NIS2**, the registry can cancel a domain if the registrant's contact data is inaccurate. Use your real details.

**Verdict:** a person living in India can probably register a .si through Hostinger. Confirm at checkout. It's still the wrong extension for an AI brand.

## 4. Recommendation: top 3

### #1 AskHemant (recommended)
- **Domains:** askhemant.ai (primary) · askhemant.com (cheap redirect, often free for year 1 with Hostinger hosting) · askhemant.in (optional, ~₹899/yr renewal) · askhemant.si (skip)
- **IG:** **@askhemant** · backups **@askhemant.ai**, **@hemantautomates**
- **Why:** the name is also the call to action ("Ask Hemant how to automate your follow-ups"), which suits reel hooks, DM keywords and a "Book a call" link. For a solo consultant with no case studies yet, buyers are choosing a person, so a personal brand builds trust faster *(judgement, not a statistic)*. No conflicts found, and it works in India and abroad.
- **Risks:**
  - Some people will hear "Hemanth" (a common South Indian spelling). A cheap optional fix is to redirect askhemanth.com *(optional)*.
  - The brand is tied to one person. If Hemant later builds a team or product, they can launch Kramik or AgentKaar alongside it ("Kramik, by AskHemant").

### #2 Kramik
- **Domains:** kramik.ai · kramik.in · kramik.si · kramik.com (a 6-letter .com is likely already registered; *estimate, unverified*)
- **IG:** **@kramik.ai** · backups **@kramikai**, **@kramik.hq**
- **Why:** a short, invented-sounding 6-letter name with a true story: क्रमिक means "step-by-step / in sequence", which is exactly what a workflow is. It's mostly spelled the way it sounds, reads as a credible B2B brand, and can scale into an agency or product.
- **Risks:** the meaning isn't obvious abroad, so it needs a tagline ("AI automation, step by step"). Some people may mishear it as "Kramick" or "Cramik". There's a mild sound-alike in Kramate (India).

### #3 AgentKaar

- **IG:** **@agentkaar** · backups **@agentkaar.ai**, **@agentkaar.hq**

## 5. Instagram handle rules

Handles can be up to 30 characters, using only letters, numbers, periods and underscores. No double periods, and no period at the start or end. Handles are case-insensitive. The display name is separate and can include spaces, so a good format is **"Hemant | AI Automation Consultant"**. Prefer a period over an underscore in backups (@askhemant.ai) because it reads like the domain. Changing a handle later breaks old tags and links, so pick once.

## 6. 5-minute verification checklist (before paying)

1. **Hostinger:** search each domain at hostinger.com/domain-name-search. Check that the price isn't flagged "premium".
2. **WHOIS:** check lookup.icann.org for the .com and .ai.
3. **Instagram:** in the app, go to Edit profile → Username and type each handle (the check is instant).
4. **Trademark:** search IP India's public search and USPTO for each name in **Class 35** (business consulting) and **Class 42** (software/SaaS).
5. **Google exact match:** search `"askhemant"` and `"kramik"` in quotes.
6. **Order:** grab the IG handle and the domains on the same day, then verify the registrant email Hostinger sends.
