| **2. Productised offer (fixed-price ladder, short funnel)** | **9** | **8** | **8** | **9** | **10** | **8.8 — winner** |
| 3. Personal brand / content-first (broad, data picks the niche) | 6 | 8 | 6 | 9 | 7 | **7.2** |

**Why Strategy 2 wins:**
- It is the only plan that does the arithmetic and admits that a new Instagram account cannot fill a calendar by itself. It budgets one-to-one outreach to make up the difference.
- Its founding offer has a real deadline.
- It includes a "Week 0 exit check": a full test run from comment to payment.
- It gives exact copy for the bio, the DMs and the call script.

**Where the runners-up were stronger:**
- **Strategy 1:** the sharpest ideal-client definition, the Team tier, a demo library, and human review of AI conversations.
- **Strategy 3:** one build session per week feeding all five posts; tagging each post with a niche "lane" so data decides the niche; and an AI-product rung that matches Hemant's real range of skills.
- **Strategy 1's weakness:** it overloads week 1 (a 15-template kit, a scorecard, a JSON workflow and 8 demos).
- **Strategy 3's weakness:** broad positioning delays the first sale, and the account risks attracting learners instead of buyers.

### Ideas taken from the runners-up into the final plan

## 1. Positioning

**Positioning statement**
> AskHemant is for **real-estate brokers and channel partners in India and the UAE, and appointment-led private clinics**, who lose enquiries because nobody replies fast enough or follows up.
>
> **Hemant** is a hands-on AI automation consultant who installs a **fixed-price, WhatsApp-first lead system**. Every enquiry gets an instant reply, is qualified by an AI assistant scoped to the business, gets a site visit or appointment booked and reminded, receives 30 days of follow-up, and is logged in a sheet or CRM. Typical go-live is 10 working days.
>
> Other options leave the work to the owner. CRMs, WhatsApp platforms and Meta's free WhatsApp Business AI all need setting up, and agencies quote vague custom projects. Hemant **publishes prices**, builds on the **client's own accounts**, and **measures response time before and after**.

**One-line value proposition**
> Every lead answered within a minute, qualified and booked for a site visit or appointment: a fixed-price AI + WhatsApp system, live in 10 working days.

- "Within a minute" is the **design target**, measured from the moment a lead reaches the system. It is not a measured client result yet.
- Some sources add delay before the lead reaches the system, such as portal notification emails.
- Never promise deals or revenue.

**Tagline:** **No lead left on read.** Sign-off line: *Ask Hemant. Automate the rest.*

**Pitch for DMs and calls (about 20 seconds)**
> "I build the system that replies to your property leads on WhatsApp within a minute, asks the qualifying questions, books the site visit and follows up for 30 days, so you stop losing leads while you're out on visits. It's a fixed price, live in 10 working days, and built on your own accounts."

**Why this position is open** (market research, `research/market-competitors.md`)
- **The big AI-automation creators teach would-be agency owners, not business owners.** Liam Ottley has about 900K YouTube subscribers but only about 41K Instagram followers. No one clearly owns "AI automation for Indian business owners" on Instagram.
- **Meta launched Business AI (May 2026) and Meta Business Agent (July 2026) on WhatsApp for Indian SMBs** ([Deccan Herald](https://www.deccanherald.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/whatsapp-gets-gen-ai-powered-meta-business-agent-for-small-enterprise-owners-in-india-4065355), [UNI](https://www.uniindia.com/whatsapp-unveils-meta-business-agent-for-businesses-in-india/business-economy/news/3902347.html)).
  - That makes basic FAQ bots close to free.
  - So Hemant **never sells "a bot"**. Hemant sells **the connected system**: every lead source, qualification, handover to a human, booking, follow-up and the CRM.
- **People are tired of hype.** A widely shared critique is titled "AI agencies charge $5K a client. Few get five." ([Medium](https://medium.com/@automation.labs/ai-automation-agencies-charge-5k-a-client-few-get-five-f0e9ad0b5dde)). Published prices and labelled demos stand out against that.

**Words to use:**
- India: enquiry, site visit, hot lead, BHK, budget, locality, possession, channel partner, portal leads, Meta leads, follow-up.
- Dubai: viewing, off-plan, listing.

**Words to keep out of hooks:** agentic, LLM, RAG, orchestration, "10x", "$10K/month".

## 2. Target client

### Primary: real estate (India first, Dubai/UAE from month 2)

| | India (start here) | Dubai / UAE (from month 2) |
|---|---|---|
| **Who** | RERA-registered solo brokers and teams of 1–10; channel partners selling builder projects; small developers' sales desks | Brokers at independent and boutique agencies; off-plan specialists |
| **Fit signals** | Runs Meta lead ads and/or pays for 99acres, MagicBricks or Housing.com listings; at least 2 lead sources; the owner or a junior answers WhatsApp by hand; roughly **40–500 enquiries a month** *(estimate threshold)* | Active on Instagram; works in English; heavy flow of leads from Property Finder, Bayut, Dubizzle and ads |
| **Don't take** | Fewer than about 20 leads a month (too few to show a return); large developers (long procurement) | Large brokerages with in-house CRM teams |
| **Market size** | About **105,712 RERA-registered agents** in 2025 ([Realty Today](https://therealtytoday.com/news/trending/indias-rera-registered-real-estate-agents-grow-19-in-2025-cross-100000-mark-exp-realty-india-report/)) | **32,294 registered brokers** at the end of 2025, 13,083 of them new that year ([WAM](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/bz486m3-brokerage-sector-strengthens-role-key-driver)) |
| **Why now** | Owners are hearing "AI on WhatsApp" because of Meta's 2026 launches, and they need someone to connect it to their lead flow | Dubai is 1.5 hours behind IST, so working hours overlap almost completely; international prices apply |

**Hypothesis to test on calls:** NRI buyers in the Gulf, UK and US who enquire about Indian projects outside Indian business hours. This is a natural "your system replies while you sleep" story, and it links both markets.

### Top pains, with how strong the evidence is

| # | Pain | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The first reply is slow.** Leads go cold while the broker is on a site visit, driving or asleep | Strong. An audit of 2,241 US firms found only about 37% answered web leads within an hour, and 23% never answered ([BYU ScholarsArchive / HBR](https://scholarsarchive.byu.edu/facpub/9711)). Only 3% of marketers reply to a new lead within 5 minutes ([NP Digital, Feb 2026](https://neilpatel.com/marketing-stats/response-time-new-leads/)). India-specific data: Hemant measures it (§8) |
| 2 | Leads are scattered across portals, Meta forms, Instagram DMs, WhatsApp and calls, with no single list | Vendor framing ([Sell.do](https://www.sell.do/blog/whatsapp-first-real-estate-2026-playbook)). Confirm on calls |
| 3 | Junk or unqualified Meta-ad leads waste calling time | Hypothesis. Measure it in pilots |
| 4 | Nobody follows up after day 2, and site visits are missed without reminders | Hypothesis. Measure it in pilots; don't quote a statistic |
| 5 | Juniors reply inconsistently, and the owner can't see what happened to each lead | Hypothesis (teams and channel partners) |

### Where they hang out
- **Instagram.** Agents post listings and reels there.
  - India has the world's largest Instagram audience, about 481M users ([Statista](https://www.statista.com/statistics/578364/countries-with-most-instagram-users/)).
  - 68% of US Realtors use Instagram for business (NAR 2023, via [Luxury Presence](https://www.luxurypresence.com/blogs/real-estate-social-media-statistics/)).
- **WhatsApp groups** for brokers and builder channel partners. Join only with the admin's permission, and never post promotions.
- **Facebook groups** for local brokers, and **LinkedIn**, especially for Dubai brokerages and developers.
- **Real-estate sales trainers** on YouTube and Instagram. These are collab targets.
- **Offline:** builders' channel-partner meets, property expos, local broker associations.
- **Portals they pay for:** 99acres, MagicBricks, Housing.com, Property Finder, Bayut. These are systems to connect to, not places to market.

### Secondary markets
1. **Private clinics** (dental, dermatology/aesthetic, physio; 1–10 doctors).
   - **Pains:** missed calls during procedures and lunch, no-shows, and no recall or review process. Planet DDS reports a **6.9% average dental no-show rate and 12.9% cancellations** in 2025 ([DOCS Education](https://www.docseducation.com/node/53377)).
   - **Offer:** the *Missed-Call & No-Show Recovery Sprint*. It reuses most of the real-estate build.
   - **Care needed:** India's DPDP Act covers health data. Use only the official WhatsApp Business Platform.
2. **Digital-marketing agencies** that run Meta ads for brokers or clinics.
   - **Size:** Goodfirms lists 11,424 Indian agencies ([Goodfirms](https://www.goodfirms.co/directory/country/top-digital-marketing-companies/in)).
   - **How it works:** a white-label partner channel from month 2, once one real case study exists.

### Don't serve (this protects the calendar)
- **Restaurants and salons:** budgets are thin, and software already covers these needs.
- **"General ChatGPT on WhatsApp" requests:** Meta has banned general-purpose AI chatbots on the WhatsApp Business Platform since 15 Jan 2026 ([respond.io](https://respond.io/blog/whatsapp-general-purpose-chatbots-ban)).
- **Bulk or cold WhatsApp blasting.**
- **Budgets under about ₹15K:** send these people to the free Kit or the paid Session.

### Format rules

- **Search:**

## 10. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Early warning sign | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Meta's free WhatsApp AI makes basic bots a commodity | "Why pay you?" on calls | Sell the connected system (sources, qualification, handover, booking, 30-day follow-up, CRM), not "a bot". Use Meta's free tools inside the system where they fit. Publish the honest comparison (post #4) |
| WhatsApp policy breach or a banned number | A template is rejected or the number is flagged | Official WhatsApp Business Platform or a BSP only. Every bot scoped to the client's business (general-purpose AI bots banned since 15 Jan 2026). Recorded opt-in, approved templates, no broadcasts to cold lists |
| Instagram spam or DM-automation blocks | Action blocks; DMs not delivered | Follow Meta's private-reply rules. No cold mass DMs. Pitch only after a real exchange. Tell people to "check your message requests" |
| A new account grows slowly | Under 150 followers at day 30 | About half of calls come from direct outreach. Revenue doesn't depend on followers. Trial reels, collabs, LinkedIn and Shorts cross-posting |
| Attracting learners instead of buyers | Most sign-ups are freelancers or students | Hooks in owner language. Business type and budget questions on the forms. Do-it-yourselfers go to the paid Session, not free calls. No n8n JSON in the lead magnet |
| Real estate is the wrong niche | One lane gets no response by day 45 | Niche-neutral brand and domain. 60/20/20 mix. Lane tags. The day-60 decision rule. Clinics next |
| Portal leads can't be captured reliably | No API; delayed alert emails | Confirm every source in the Audit before quoting. "Within a minute" counts only from when the lead reaches the system |
| Weak AI quality in Hinglish | Wrong or awkward replies | Human review of the first 100 conversations. Handover when confidence is low. No voice AI at launch |
| Underpricing and scope creep | Projects run 2x over the estimate | Fixed scope plus an out-of-scope list. A change-request rate. 50% upfront. No more than 2 Sprints at once. Price step at day 90 |
| Data protection (DPDP Rules notified Nov 2025; notice and consent from about Nov 2026; most duties by about May 2027) | Data collected without a notice | Privacy notice and an unticked consent box now ([Legal500](https://www.legal500.com/firms/237811-saga-legal/c-india/news-and-developments/the-privacy-timer-starts-now-analysing-the-dpdp-rules-and-its-18-month-countdown)). Client-owned accounts, a processor clause, minimal data, deletion at the end of a contract. Extra care with clinics |
| Payments, foreign exchange and tax | KYC delays; Wise or Stripe problems | Razorpay and Skydo KYC in week 1. GST registration becomes compulsory above ₹20 lakh a year **including exports**, and an LUT for zero-rated exports needs registration ([Skydo](https://www.skydo.com/blog/gst-for-freelancers)). See a CA before the first foreign invoice |
| Over-claiming | Comments asking "which client?" | DEMO labels everywhere. Sources on screen. Only consented, measured numbers, with sample sizes |
| Domain or handle already taken | The availability check fails | Backups in §3. Register everything on the same day |
| Solo burnout | Missed posting days | One Saturday batch session, reusable build templates, 6 calls a week at most, 2 Sprints at once at most. Drop to 3 posts a week, never zero |
| Losing the Instagram account | Login or security alerts | 2FA with an authenticator app, backup codes, an owned email and WhatsApp opt-in list, and the website as the hub |
| Conflict of interest from referral commissions | — | Disclose any commission and recommend tools on merit |

## 11. Assumptions and open questions for Hemant

**Assumptions:**
1. Hemant works solo, is based in India and is budget-conscious.
2. Hemant has no audience and no public case studies yet.
3. ".si" was a typo for ".ai". Even if it wasn't, .ai is still recommended.
4. Hemant can build production-quality n8n/Make workflows, WhatsApp Cloud API or BSP integrations, and GPT/Claude agents.
5. Hemant is comfortable on camera in English and Hindi/Hinglish. If not, the content can lean on screen recordings with voiceover.
6. Hemant can put in about 25–40 hours a week across content, outreach and delivery.
7. Hemant is happy to publish prices and offer the founding discount.
8. Hemant has about ₹34–52K for the full stack, or about ₹9–15K for the lean path.
9. Hemant is not yet GST-registered and will consult a CA.

**Open questions:**
1. Did you mean **.ai** or really **.si**? Is there any Slovenia or EU angle?
2. Which **city** are you in? This decides the local real-estate market, where field tests run, and whether you can meet clients in person.
3. How many **hours a week** can you give this: full-time, or alongside a job?
4. What have you **already built** (n8n, WhatsApp API, voice agents, CRMs, AI products)? Can any of it be shown, with permission?
5. Do you personally know any **brokers, channel partners, clinic owners or agency owners**? They are the fastest route to founding clients.
6. How comfortable are you **on camera**, and in **which languages** besides English and Hindi?
7. Are you **GST-registered**, or do you have a business entity? Do you have a CA?
8. Which **budget**: the full stack (about ₹34–52K) or the lean path (about ₹9–15K)?
9. Are you comfortable with **published prices** and the **₹14,999 / $599 founding price**?
10. Do you want to stay a **personal brand** long term, or grow into an agency or product brand later (Kramik or AgentKaar kept in reserve)?
11. Which **time zones** can you take calls in (UAE, UK, US East)?
12. Should anything stay **private**: your face, your city, your employer?
