## 2. The daily routine: 40 minutes of engagement + 10 personal messages

Every day from Mon 19 Oct. Personal messages: 5 a day in week 1 (from Thu 22 Oct), **10 a day from Mon 26 Oct** (Mon–Fri as the base; Saturday optional).

### 2.1 The 40 minutes

| Min | Do | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **0–10** | **Reply to every comment** on today's and yesterday's posts (a real answer or a question back). Check that every LEAKS/PRICE comment got its auto-DM in the Business Suite inbox. | Reply in the first hour after posting when you can. Never argue; answer objections with the honest line (e.g. "Meta's free WhatsApp AI is great for FAQs. What it doesn't do is …") |
| **10–15** | **DMs and message requests.** Everyone who answered the auto-DM question gets your **personal reply** (the script in [`instagram/07-dm-automation-playbook.md`](../instagram/07-dm-automation-playbook.md) / strategy §5) **inside Meta's 24-hour window**, ideally within 4 working hours. Then WhatsApp Business app: new chats, labels. | One concrete idea first, then the Fit Call offer ("I'll only pitch if it's a fit"). Follow-ups only within 24 hours of their reply |
| **15–25** | **Comment on 10–20 target accounts** from your lists (Indian brokers, Dubai agents, real-estate marketers, clinics) in [`sales/11-warm-outreach-playbook.md`](../sales/11-warm-outreach-playbook.md). | Specific and useful ("If these enquiries come from Meta forms, an instant WhatsApp first reply would…"). No links, no pitch, no "great post" |
| **25–40** | **10 personal one-to-one messages** to warm contacts or people who engaged (liked, commented, replied to a Story, downloaded the kit). | See 2.2. **No cold mass DMs**, no broadcast lists, no WhatsApp groups without the admin's permission |
| **+5** | **Log it:** every new conversation is a row in the pipeline Sheet (source, keyword, lane, business type, stage). Count messages sent today. | The scorecard can only be as good as this log |

If the block runs over, cut target-account comments to 10 first. **Never cut lead replies.**

### 2.2 The 10 personal messages

- **Who counts:** people who know you; people who know brokers, channel partners, clinic owners or agency owners; people who engaged with a post, a Story or the kit. Not strangers from a scraped list.
- **What to send:** the scripts in [`sales/11-warm-outreach-playbook.md`](../sales/11-warm-outreach-playbook.md). A personal first line, one specific reason you're writing, one easy question or offer (e.g. a free, consented **Speed Check**: with their permission you send a test enquiry and give them the result plus a 2-minute video of how it could be answered within a minute, strategy §8).
- **Follow-up:** at most one, 3–4 days later, and only where a follow-up is normal (people who know you, or inside a live conversation).
- **Count two numbers** (§6): messages sent this week (target 50) and **new people** you contacted for the first time (the strategy's cumulative outreach KPI: 100 by day 30, 250 by day 60, 400 by day 90).

### 2.3 Stories (5+ days a week, about 10 minutes)
Poll (Mon, Thu), question box (Tue, Sun), behind the scenes (Wed), /book link sticker (Fri), weekly recap (Sat). Story prompts and Highlight rules: [`instagram/05-stories-broadcast-collabs.md`](../instagram/05-stories-broadcast-collabs.md).

## 5. Sales and delivery rules

### 5.1 Calls and proposals
- **Fit Call:** free, 20 minutes, intake form required, Tue–Thu only, **max 6 a week**. Script: [`sales/01-fit-call-script.md`](../sales/01-fit-call-script.md). Minute 0–2 frame, 2–10 diagnose, 10–15 name the 1–2 biggest leaks and recommend, 15–20 price and timeline.
- **Routing:** a budget under ₹15K, or a real-estate or clinic business with under 20 leads a month → the Kit and the **₹1,999 / $99 Ask Hemant Session** instead of a Fit Call. Any other business with a budget of ₹15K or more keeps the Fit Call; the likely outcome is a Quick Win Build or an AI Build Scoping ([`sales/02-cal-com-intake-form.md`](../sales/02-cal-com-intake-form.md)).
- **Same day:** when the next step is an Audit, send the Audit payment link on WhatsApp **during the call**.
- **Within 24 hours:** otherwise, a 1-page proposal with 2 options (Sprint, or Sprint + Care), a 3-minute Loom, the out-of-scope list, the running-cost estimate, and 7-day validity ([`sales/03-proposal-template.md`](../sales/03-proposal-template.md)). Follow-ups: [`sales/07-follow-up-templates.md`](../sales/07-follow-up-templates.md).
- **Show-rate protection:** a WhatsApp reminder 2 hours before every call ("Reply 1 to confirm"), on top of Cal.com's emails.

### 5.2 Delivery (strategy §5)
- **No more than 2 Sprints at once.** A third signed client gets a start date, not a parallel build.
- **The clock starts when the access checklist is complete** ([`sales/06-client-onboarding-checklist.md`](../sales/06-client-onboarding-checklist.md)). **Start Meta business verification and template approval on day 1**: they set the timeline.
- **Sprint timeline:** day 5 demo to the client · day 10 go-live · day 24 the warranty ends and Lead Desk Care is offered.
- **On-time promise:** if a Sprint isn't live within 10 working days of full access, the client gets the first month of Care free.
- **Quality:** a human (you) reviews the **first 100 AI conversations** in every pilot; hand over to a human when confidence is low.
- **All client accounts and running costs in the client's own name** (WhatsApp provider, Meta, AI API, CRM). Never ask for passwords; use partner access.
- **Founding pilots:** the build period is the baseline measurement; measure again 30 days after go-live; publish only with written permission, never rounded up ([`sales/08-case-study-template-and-consent.md`](../sales/08-case-study-template-and-consent.md), [`sales/10-founding-client-program.md`](../sales/10-founding-client-program.md)).
- **Weekly delivery block:** schedule build hours in the calendar on Monday for the whole week (mornings plus the non-call afternoons); calls never displace a go-live date.

### 5.3 Integrity rules (every week, no exceptions)
- "DEMO – fictional business" on screen and in the caption of every demo.
- Publish only numbers you measured, with the method and n. Vendor statistics are cited and never presented as your results.
- No testimonials, client names, logos or results without written permission; no follower or income claims; no fake scarcity (the only deadlines are the real ones: founding close Mon 30 Nov 2026, price rise Mon 18 Jan 2027 if announced).
- Field tests: genuine-style enquiries only where enquiring is normal; never break a portal's terms; never book a fake visit; disclose afterwards; publish aggregates only.

## 7. Decision rules

### 7.1 Day-28 review (Sun 15 Nov, 90 minutes)
1. Score week 4 against the day-30 column.
2. Look at average watch time, sends per reach, non-follower reach, LEAKS comments per post, and kit sign-ups and calls **by lane**.
3. **Keep the 2 best topics; drop the weakest format.**
4. **DM tool:** stay on Meta's native Comment to Message, move to **ManyChat Essential** (rule: more than 25 people a month comment, since ManyChat Free stops at 25 contacts), or build your own n8n Instagram flow ([`03-accounts-setup-guide.md`](./03-accounts-setup-guide.md) §19).
5. **Replace the planning conversion rates** (§4) with your real ones and re-check whether the day-60 targets still need the same outreach volume.
6. **Followers under 150?** Don't panic: revenue doesn't depend on followers. Keep the 10 daily messages; lean on trial reels, the first collab, LinkedIn and Shorts.
7. Book the first collab for month 2.

### 7.2 Day-46 check (Thu 3 Dec, 30 minutes)
Count qualified conversations and kit sign-ups **by lane**. Shift next week's content towards the leading lane (e.g. 3 RE → 2 RE + 2 CL if clinics lead). Dubai: content twice a week in month 2.

### 7.3 Day-60 niche decision (Thu 17 Dec, 60 minutes)
Use the lane columns of the scorecard (qualified conversations RE / CL / GEN, kit sign-ups by lane, paid deals).

| If… | Then… |
|---|---|
| One lane has **at least 2× the qualified conversations of the next lane**, **or** has produced the **first paid non-pilot deal** | **Commit:** rewrite the Instagram bio and name field, the site headline ([`website/index.html`](../website/index.html)) and pinned post #3; give that lane **80% of content** |
| Nothing converts | **Switch to clinics** rather than going broader |
| The data is unclear | **Stay on real estate** |
| In every case | Agencies remain a resale (white-label) channel at list price −25% on builds (**Decision for Hemant:** whether it also applies to the Audit, add-ons and retainers; [`sales/04-pricing-sheet.md`](../sales/04-pricing-sheet.md) §5) |

### 7.4 The Fit Call switch rule (check weekly; decide by day 60)
If **any** of these is true by day 60:
- more than **8 Fit Calls a week**, or
- a **show rate below 70%**, or
- **3+ documented results** exist,

then either **shorten the Fit Call to 15 minutes**, or **make the Audit (₹9,999 / $499) the only way in**. Update the Cal.com event ([`03-accounts-setup-guide.md`](./03-accounts-setup-guide.md) §9), the site's /book page and [`sales/02-cal-com-intake-form.md`](../sales/02-cal-com-intake-form.md). Until then, the Fit Call stays free and gated, capped at 6 a week.

### 7.5 Price rise (count Sun 3 Jan; announce Mon 4 Jan; effective Mon 18 Jan 2027)
- **Condition:** at least **3 documented results** (definition in §6.2). Count them on **Sun 3 Jan**.
- **If met:** announce on **Mon 4 Jan 2027**; new prices from **Mon 18 Jan 2027**: **Audit ₹14,999 / $699; Solo Sprint ₹44,999 / $1,999**. Proposals already sent keep their price for their 7-day validity. The deadline is real, so this is honest scarcity. On 18 Jan, also update the Audit box on the kit's last page ([`lead-magnet/src/kit.html`](../lead-magnet/src/kit.html)), re-render with `node lead-magnet/render.mjs` and redeploy ([`02-launch-checklist.md`](./02-launch-checklist.md) week 13).
- **If not met:** no announcement; prices stay; re-check at the day-90 review and monthly after that.
- **Decision for Hemant:** the strategy names only the Audit and the Solo Sprint. Decide before Mon 4 Jan whether the Clinic Sprint (₹34,999 / $1,499 today, the same as the Solo Sprint) also rises on 18 Jan 2027; until then it stays at its launch price ([`sales/04-pricing-sheet.md`](../sales/04-pricing-sheet.md) §6).

### 7.6 Day-90 review (Sat 16 Jan 2027, 2 hours)
1. Score the day-90 column; write what worked, what didn't, and why.
2. Write the **Q1 plan around the winning niche**.
3. Decide on **ManyChat Pro**, **long-form YouTube**, and a **Template Pack** (check the GST rules for automated online services sold to foreign consumers with a CA first).
4. **Meta Verified:** the default is still no.

## 8. When you're behind: the minimum viable week

Use this when delivery, illness or a festival squeezes the week. **The floor is 3 feed posts a week**: accounts that skip a week see a "no-posting penalty" ([Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/)).

| Area | Full week | Minimum viable week |
|---|---|---|
| Feed posts | 5 (3 Reels + 2 carousels) | **3**: Mon demo Reel, Wed or Fri Reel, 1 carousel (a banked evergreen or a re-captioned kit page from [`lead-magnet/pages/`](../lead-magnet/pages/)) |
| Trial reels | 2 | 0 (drop these first) |
| Stories | 5+ days | 3 days |
| Batch session | 3 hours | **90 minutes:** one build → 2 Reels + 1 carousel |
| Engagement | 40 min/day | **20 min/day: replies only** (comments, DMs, message requests, WhatsApp) |
| Personal messages | 10/day | 5/day |
| Fit Calls | up to 6 | keep the booked ones; open no extra slots |
| Proposals | within 24 h | **within 24 h (non-negotiable)** |
| Client delivery | as planned | **commitments first (non-negotiable)** |
| Sunday scorecard | 30 min | 15 min: leads and sales rows only |

**Drop in this order:** trial reels → second carousel → cross-posting → target-account comments → Story days → third Reel. **Never drop:** replies to leads inside the 24-hour window, proposal turnaround, client deadlines, the 3-post floor, the integrity rules (§5.3).

**Don'ts:** don't double-post to catch up; don't repost other people's content or AI-news clips (original content only); don't go silent for a week.

**Recovery:** next week returns to the full cadence. Note the cause in the scorecard's `notes`, and refill the 3-post evergreen bank in the next batch session.
