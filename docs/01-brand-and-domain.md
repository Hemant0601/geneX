**Why AskHemant wins:**
1. **No conflict found.** Research-phase web search found no company, product or site using "AskHemant" ([research file](./research/domain-and-name.md)).
2. **The name is the CTA.** It works in hooks ("Ask Hemant: why do my portal leads go cold?"), in DMs and on the "Book a call" button.

5. **It is easy to say and spell when heard, in India and abroad.**

**Known risks, and their fixes:**

### Comparison

| Question | **.ai** | **.si** |
|---|---|---|
| Does it say what Hemant sells? | Yes. "AI" | No. Looks like a typo, or like Slovenia |
| What happens when someone hears "askhemant dot…"? | They type .ai and reach Hemant | They type .ai anyway, and reach **whoever owns askhemant.ai** |
| Cost over 2 years *(estimate)* | ~$190–210 | ~$29 (₹1,169 + ₹1,649 ≈ ₹2.8K) |
| Risk | Cash up front | Lost traffic and credibility; a rival could take the .ai |

**Verdict:**
- **Buy askhemant.ai as the main domain and askhemant.com as a redirect. Skip .si and .co.**
- Add **.in** only if Indian ads become a channel.
- **If cash is tight:** launch on askhemant.com (free in year 1 with Premium) and buy the .ai once the first payment comes in, accepting the risk that someone registers it first.
- **If Hemant really did want .si** (for example, for an EU angle): that's open question 1 in the strategy file. It is still not recommended as the main domain.

## 4. How to buy and verify on Hostinger (step by step)

Menu labels change. Steps marked *(UI path unverified)* come from general knowledge, not the research.

**Before you pay** (also §6):
1. **Claim the Instagram handle first** (§5, step 1). It's free and instant; domains take longer.
2. Go to **hostinger.com/in** (the India store, prices in ₹) → **Domains → Domain name search**. Search `askhemant.ai` and `askhemant.com`.
   - Both should show as available at the **standard** price.
   - If either is marked **"premium"** (a much higher price), stop and use the backups.

**Buy:**

3. Go to **Hosting → Web Hosting → Premium**.
   - Choose **48 months** for the lowest monthly rate, or **12 months** to spend less cash now.
   - Create the Hostinger account with an email you will always be able to reach. Gmail is fine; you can add hello@ later.
4. During onboarding, or later under hPanel → **Domains**, **claim the free domain: `askhemant.com`**.
5. Go to **Domains → Get a new domain**. Search `askhemant.ai`, select the **2-year** term (the minimum), and add WHOIS privacy if it's offered for .ai (availability not confirmed).
6. **Registrant details:** use your **real legal name, address, phone and email**. Registries can suspend or cancel domains with false contact details; NIS2 rules apply to .si, for example.
7. Pay with an Indian card or another method available at checkout. **Download every invoice**, because they are business expenses.

**Verify:**

8. **Open the verification email Hostinger sends and click the link straight away.** Hostinger says the registrant email must be verified after registration ([Hostinger docs](https://docs.hostinger.com/domains/registering)). Unverified domains can be suspended; the exact deadline is not confirmed.
9. In hPanel → Domains, check that both domains show **Active**, that **auto-renew is ON** and that **transfer lock is ON** *(UI path unverified)*.
10. Check publicly that you own them:
    - **askhemant.com:** look it up at [lookup.icann.org](https://lookup.icann.org). It should show Hostinger as the registrar and a recent creation date.
    - **askhemant.ai:** try lookup.icann.org too. If it shows no record, use the .ai registry's own WHOIS (general knowledge).
11. Put **calendar reminders 30 days before each renewal**. The .ai renews at $109.99 a year, which is easy to miss.

**Connect:**

12. hPanel → **Websites**: make **askhemant.ai** the website's main domain *(UI path unverified)*.
13. Set **askhemant.com → 301 redirect → https://askhemant.ai** *(UI path unverified)*. Do the same for askhemanth.com if you bought it.
    - Look under hPanel → Domains → Redirect.
    - Fallback: a `.htaccess` 301 rule on the parked domain.
14. hPanel → **Security → SSL**: install SSL on both domains and **force HTTPS** ([tooling research](./research/tooling-and-ops.md); not re-verified).
15. hPanel → **Emails**: create **hello@askhemant.ai**.
    - Check that the SPF, DKIM and DMARC DNS records exist. They are usually added automatically when the domain uses Hostinger nameservers (general knowledge).
    - Send a test message to a Gmail address and make sure it doesn't land in spam.
16. Deploy the site: hPanel → **Advanced → Git → Connect with GitHub** to auto-deploy on every push ([Hostinger Git docs](https://docs.hostinger.com/websites/git)).
17. **Final check:** open `http://askhemant.com` in a private window. It should land on `https://askhemant.ai` with a padlock.

   - If it's available, the app accepts it at once.
   - **Don't save it on your personal account.** Back out and go to step 2.

4. **Secure it the same day:**
   - Two-factor authentication with an **authenticator app** (not SMS only).
   - Save the backup codes offline.
   - Confirm the email and phone.
   - The account will hold leads, so losing it would be expensive.
5. **Fill in the profile before the first post:**
   - Name field: `Hemant | AI Automation Consultant · Real Estate`.
   - The bio, 5 UTM-tagged links, email and WhatsApp contact buttons, and Highlight covers. Exact copy is in `00-strategy.md` §5.
   - **Profile photo:** Hemant's face (head and shoulders) on a **Signal Indigo** (#4338CA) background. A personal brand needs a face, not a logo.
6. **Claim the same name everywhere on the same day:**
   - **Threads** (uses the Instagram handle).
   - **YouTube** @askhemant.
   - A **Facebook Page** "AskHemant". Meta Business Suite and some DM tools have historically needed a linked Page; current requirements are unverified.
   - A **LinkedIn** custom URL on Hemant's personal profile, for example `linkedin.com/in/askhemant` or `/hemantkumar-askhemant`.
   - **X** (optional).
7. **Connect to Meta Business Suite** (business.facebook.com, **desktop**). Set up **Inbox → Automations → Comment to Message** for `LEAKS` / `PRICE` and their case variants. Test from a second account.
8. **Optional impersonation guard:** also register **@askhemant.ai**. Set it to private with the bio "Official account: @askhemant".
9. **Don't:**
   - buy handles from third parties;
   - use "official" or "verified" in the name;
   - mass-follow or mass-DM from a new account. Users report temporary blocks after aggressive automation ([research](./research/instagram-playbook.md)).

**If @askhemant is taken:** use **@askhemant.ai**. A period reads like the domain and is allowed; handles can be up to 30 characters of letters, numbers, periods and underscores, with no double periods and no period at either end ([Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/instagram-character-limit/)). Next options: **@hemantautomates**, then **@hemant.automates**. Keep the brand name "AskHemant" in the name field and on the site whichever handle you get.

## 7. Visual identity

**Design idea:**
- **"The reply that arrives first."** Every demo will be full of green WhatsApp screenshots, so the brand uses **indigo + amber**. That contrasts with the screenshots and avoids looking like WhatsApp or borrowing Meta's brand colours.
- **Amber** is the "new message" notification dot: urgency, a lead waiting.
- **Indigo** is calm competence: the system has it handled.

### Colour palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Use | Contrast (WCAG) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | Signal Indigo | **#4338CA** | Buttons, CTA slides, links, profile-photo background, logo "Hemant" | White on Indigo **7.9:1** ✅ AA/AAA; on Paper 7.25:1 |
| **Accent** | Lead Amber | **#FFB020** | Notification dot, highlights, **DEMO badge**, stopwatch, one key word per slide | Ink on Amber **10.35:1** ✅. Amber on Indigo is 4.32:1, so **large text only**. **Never put amber text on Paper or white** (1.68:1 ❌) |
| **Dark** | Ink | **#0B1020** | Main text, dark slides, Reel caption boxes | Ink on Paper **17.4:1** ✅ |
| **Light** | Paper | **#F7F5F0** | Warm off-white backgrounds for carousels and the site | — |
| Support | Slate | #5B5F73 | Secondary text, captions, source lines on slides | On Paper 5.79:1 ✅ |

### Logo and marks
- **Wordmark:** **Ask**Hemant in Space Grotesk Bold. "Ask" is set in Ink (or white on dark), and "Hemant" in Signal Indigo (or Indigo Tint on dark). A small **Lead Amber dot** sits at the top right, like an unread-message badge.
- **Avatar and favicon mark:** "AH" in a rounded chat-bubble shape (Indigo), with the amber dot.
- **On Instagram, the profile photo is Hemant's face, not the logo.** The logo appears on the site, on carousel footers and on Reel end cards.

### Content style rules
- **Signature device:** an on-screen **stopwatch** in JetBrains Mono, in an amber pill, timing every demo from "lead arrives" to "reply sent". It is the visual version of "No lead left on read."
- **DEMO badge:** on every demo frame and thumbnail, top-left. An amber pill with Ink text reading `DEMO – fictional business`. Never cropped out.
- **Carousels:**
  - 1080×1350, with a 64 px safe margin.
  - Slide 1: the hook in Ink on Paper, with one amber-highlighted word.
  - Middle slides: one idea each, at most about 30 words.
  - Sources go in Slate at 20 px at the bottom.
  - The **last slide is an Indigo CTA**: "Comment LEAKS".
- **Reel covers:** 1080×1920, with the title in the central safe zone because the grid crops thumbnails.
- **Screen recordings:**
  - Zoom in on the action (about 1.25x).
  - Hide real phone numbers and names. **Blur any real personal data** (DPDP).
  - Captions in Inter SemiBold, white, in an Ink box at 80% opacity.
- **Before → After layout:** a split screen. The left side is "Before", in Leak Red tones with a manual process and a long time. The right side is "After", in Replied Green tones with the timer.
- **Photography:** real Hemant at a real desk and a real phone. Natural light. No stock photos.
- **Banned imagery:**
  - glowing robots, brains or circuit boards;
  - fake dashboards with invented numbers;
  - "$" money shots;
  - other people's logos without permission.

## 8. Brand voice

**Personality:** a calm, practical builder who shows the work. Think of the friend who knows tech, answers straight and doesn't oversell.

| Principle | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Owner language, not tech language** | "Your portal lead waited 9 hours." "Site visit booked." "Enquiry answered." | "Agentic orchestration", "LLM-powered RAG pipeline" (in hooks) |
| **Show, then claim** | "Watch the timer." "Here's the log." "Comment LEAKS and see my own system reply." | "Revolutionary", "game-changer", "10x your sales" |
| **Numbers with sources and sample sizes** | "In a small test (n=20), 6 replied within an hour." "Planet DDS: 6.9% dental no-show rate (2025)." | Vendor statistics presented as Hemant's results; rounded-up or invented numbers |
| **Honest about limits** | "Use Meta's free AI if you only need FAQs." "This won't fix bad leads." "Here's what didn't work." | "Guaranteed ROI", "never miss a deal again", income claims |
| **Published prices, no games** | "₹34,999. Here's exactly what's included and what isn't." | "DM for price", fake countdowns, fake scarcity (only real deadlines such as the 30 Nov founding close) |
| **Warm and bilingual** | Natural Hinglish for India: "Site visit pe ho aur nayi lead aa gayi?" Clean English for Dubai and international posts | Forced slang, mixing languages within a single on-screen line, talking down to people |
| **Respect privacy and consent** | "Shared with permission." "Anonymised." | Screenshots of real customers' chats; naming or shaming businesses from field tests |

**Words to use:**
- **Real estate:** enquiry, site visit, hot lead, follow-up, BHK, locality, channel partner, portal leads.
- **Clinics:** appointment, no-show, missed call, recall.
- **Everyone:** fixed price, live in 10 working days, your own accounts.

**Signature phrases:**
- *No lead left on read.*
- *Ask Hemant. Automate the rest.*
- *Watch the timer.*
- *Here's the real number.*
- *DEMO – fictional business.*

**Example lines:**
- **Hook:** "This lead came in at 11:42 pm. Watch who replies first."
- **Caption opener (keyword-rich):** "WhatsApp automation for real estate brokers: here's how an enquiry gets answered in under a minute (DEMO)."
- **CTA:** "Comment LEAKS and I'll DM you the Lead Leak Kit. Check your message requests."
- **Objection reply:** "Fair question. Meta's free WhatsApp AI is great for FAQs. What it doesn't do is pull in your portal leads, book the visit on your calendar and follow up on day 7. That's the part I build."

**Checklist before publishing anything:**
1. Is every demo labelled?
2. Is every statistic sourced?
3. Is every result measured and consented?
4. Is the price shown?
5. Is there exactly one CTA?
